## The Location Master: IN, ON, AT... It's All EN!

---

## The Big Picture

### What Does EN Mean?

**EN is the Swiss Army knife of Spanish prepositions!**

In English we use three different words:

- **IN** - in the house
- **ON** - on the table
- **AT** - at school

**In Spanish, EN does ALL THREE jobs!**

**💡 Think of it this way:** EN = "at/in/on a place or time"

---

## 1️⃣ EN for Location (IN/ON/AT)

### The Main Rule

**Use EN when something is IN, ON, or AT a place**

### Examples - All use EN!

**= IN:**

1. **Estoy en casa**

   - (I'm in the house)

2. **El gato está en la caja**

   - (The cat is in the box)

3. **Vivo en Valencia**

   - (I live in Valencia)

4. **Los peces están en el agua**
   - (The fish are in the water)

**= ON:** 5. **El libro está en la mesa**

- (The book is on the table)

6. **El pájaro está en el árbol**

   - (The bird is on the tree)

7. **La comida está en el plato**
   - (The food is on the plate)

**= AT:** 8. **Estoy en el trabajo**

- (I'm at work)

9. **Los niños están en la escuela**

   - (The children are at school)

10. **Te veo en la parada del autobús**
    - (I see you at the bus stop)

---

### Everyday Locations with EN

1. **en casa** - at home
2. **en la ciudad** - in the city
3. **en el parque** - in/at the park
4. **en la playa** - at/on the beach
5. **en la oficina** - at the office
6. **en el restaurante** - at/in the restaurant
7. **en la calle** - on/in the street
8. **en el cine** - at the movies
9. **en la tienda** - at/in the store
10. **en el hotel** - at/in the hotel

## 2️⃣ EN for Time (Months, Years, Seasons)

### Time Expressions with EN

**Use EN for:**

- Months: en enero, en marzo
- Seasons: en verano, en invierno
- Years: en 2024, en 1990
- Centuries: en el siglo XX

**Examples:**

1. **Mi cumpleaños es en julio**

   - (My birthday is in July)

2. **Hace calor en verano**

   - (It's hot in summer)

3. **Nací en 1990**

   - (I was born in 1990)

4. **En primavera, los pájaros cantan**

   - (In spring, birds sing)

5. **Empiezo el curso en septiembre**
   - (I start the course in September)

---

### Important: NOT with days or hours!

**Don't use EN with:**

- Days: el lunes ❌ NOT "en lunes"
- Hours: a las tres ❌ NOT "en las tres"

**Correct:**

- Voy **el** lunes (I go on Monday)
- Voy **a las** tres (I go at 3 o'clock)

## 3️⃣ EN for Transportation

### The Transportation Rule

**Use EN when you're INSIDE the vehicle**

**EN + vehicle:**

- en coche (by/in car)
- en autobús (by/in bus)
- en tren (by/in train)
- en avión (by/in plane)
- en metro (by/in subway)
- en taxi (by/in taxi)
- en barco (by/in boat)

**Examples:**

1. **Voy al trabajo en autobús**

   - (I go to work by bus)

2. **Viajo a Madrid en tren**

   - (I travel to Madrid by train)

3. **Vamos de vacaciones en avión**

   - (We go on vacation by plane)

4. **El pescado viaja en barco**
   - (The fish travels by boat)

---

### Exception: A PIE / A CABALLO

**NOT EN for these:**

- a pie (on foot / walking)
- a caballo (on horseback)

**Why?** Because you're ON TOP of them, not INSIDE!

**Examples:**

- Voy **a pie** al parque (I go to the park on foot)
- Los vaqueros viajan **a caballo** (Cowboys travel on horseback)

---

### Transportation Examples

1. **Voy a la escuela en bici**

   - (I go to school by bike)

2. **Viajamos en coche**

   - (We travel by car)

3. **Los turistas llegan en avión**

   - (Tourists arrive by plane)

4. **Prefiero ir en metro**

   - (I prefer to go by subway)

5. **Voy al trabajo a pie**
   - (I go to work on foot) - exception!

## 5️⃣ Tricky Cases - When NOT to Use EN

### English says "ON" but Spanish uses something else

**English: ON (day of week) = Spanish: Article only**

- ❌ en lunes
- ✅ **el** lunes (on Monday)

**English: ON (date) = Spanish: Article only**

- ❌ en el 15 de marzo
- ✅ **el** 15 de marzo (on March 15th)

**English: ON (top of) = Spanish: SOBRE/ENCIMA DE**

- ❌ en la mesa (if you mean "on top")
- ✅ **sobre** la mesa / **encima de** la mesa

---

### English says "AT" but Spanish uses A

**AT (specific time) = A**

- ❌ en las tres
- ✅ **a las** tres (at 3 o'clock)

**AT (direction/goal) = A**

- ❌ Voy en casa
- ✅ Voy **a** casa (I go home - direction)

---

## 6️⃣ EN vs A - The Big Decision

### Quick Decision Guide

**EN = Being somewhere (static)**

- Estoy **en** casa (I'm at home - I'm already there)
- Trabajo **en** la oficina (I work at the office - location)

**A = Going somewhere (movement/direction)**

- Voy **a** casa (I go home - moving toward)
- Voy **a** la oficina (I go to the office - direction)

---

### Side-by-Side Examples

| EN (static location)   | A (movement/direction) |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Estoy **en** el parque | Voy **a**l parque      |
| (I'm at the park)      | (I go to the park)     |
| Estoy **en** casa      | Voy **a** casa         |
| (I'm at home)          | (I go home)            |
| Trabajo **en** Madrid  | Viajo **a** Madrid     |
| (I work in Madrid)     | (I travel to Madrid)   |

**The Test:** Are you moving toward it? → Use A
Already there? → Use EN

## 8️⃣ Special Uses of EN

### EN + language

**Talking/writing in a language:**

- Hablo **en español** (I speak in Spanish)
- Está escrito **en inglés** (It's written in English)

### EN + manner/way

**In a certain way:**

- **En voz alta** (out loud)
- **En voz baja** (quietly)
- **En serio** (seriously)
- **En broma** (jokingly)
