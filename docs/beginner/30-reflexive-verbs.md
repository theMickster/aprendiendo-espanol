# 🎯 Part 1: Foundations

## What Are Reflexive Verbs? 🪞

Imagine looking in a mirror. When YOU do something to YOURSELF, that's reflexive!

```
Normal verb:    Yo lavo el coche.     → I wash the car.
Reflexive verb: Yo ME lavo.           → I wash MYSELF.
```

The action bounces back to YOU like a mirror! 🪞✨

## The Reflexive Pronoun Pattern

| Person          | Pronoun | Think of it as...     |
| --------------- | ------- | --------------------- |
| Yo              | **me**  | "myself"              |
| Tú              | **te**  | "yourself"            |
| Él/Ella/Usted   | **se**  | "himself/herself"     |
| Nosotros        | **nos** | "ourselves"           |
| **Vosotros** 🇪🇸 | **os**  | "yourselves" (Spain!) |
| Ellos/Ustedes   | **se**  | "themselves"          |

💡 **Living in Spain tip:** You'll hear "vosotros/os" every day in Valencia! In Latin America they use "ustedes" instead, but here in España, ¡usamos vosotros!

## 🔊 Pronunciation Guide — Tricky Sounds

### The Sounds That Trip You Up

| Verb                | Tricky Part | Say It Like...        | 🔊 Breakdown                |
| ------------------- | ----------- | --------------------- | --------------------------- |
| des**p**er**t**arse | p + t       | dess-pehr-**TAR**-seh | The "t" touches your teeth! |
| du**ch**arse        | ch          | doo-**CHAR**-seh      | Like "ch" in "chocolate"    |
| **ce**pillarse      | ce          | **THEH**-pee-YAR-seh  | 🇪🇸 Like "th" in "think"     |
| ma**qu**illarse     | qu          | ma-**KEE**-YAR-seh    | The "u" is silent!          |
| **v**estirse        | v           | **B**ess-TEER-seh     | 🇪🇸 Spanish "v" = "b" sound! |
| a**rr**eglarse      | rr          | a-**RRRR**eh-GLAR-seh | Roll that "r"! 🥁           |
| re**u**nirse        | eu          | reh-oo-NEER-seh       | Say "eh-oo" smoothly        |

### 🇪🇸 The Famous Spanish "C" and "Z"

In Valencia (and all of Spain), **c** before **e/i** and **z** sound like English "th":

| Written        | Say it              | Example                |
| -------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **ce**pillarse | **THE**-pee-yar-seh | Me cepillo los dientes |
| comen**z**ar   | co-men-**THAR**     | Vamos a comenzar       |
| al**z**arse    | al-**THAR**-seh     | to rise up             |

⚠️ **Watch out!** Your friends from Mexico might say "seh-pee-yar-seh" — but here in Valencia, we use the "th" sound!

### The "LL" Sound 🔑

In Spain, **ll** sounds like English "y":

| Written      | Say it         | Meaning      |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------ |
| **ll**amarse | **ya**-MAR-seh | to be called |
| ca**ll**arse | ca-**YAR**-seh | to be quiet  |

### Stress Patterns 🎵

Reflexive infinitives stress the syllable BEFORE "-se":

| Verb           | Stress Pattern      |
| -------------- | ------------------- |
| levantar**SE** | leh-ban-**TAR**-seh |
| dormir**SE**   | dor-**MEER**-seh    |
| vestir**SE**   | bess-**TEER**-seh   |

# ☀️ Part 2: Daily Routines

## ⭐ Level 1: Morning Wake-Up 🌅

_La mañana — Beginner_

|     | Español                                                           | English                                 |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| ⏰  | **Mamá:** ¡Buenos días! ¿A qué hora **te despiertas**?            | Good morning! What time do you wake up? |
| ⏰  | **Hija 1:** **Me despierto** a las siete.                         | I wake up at seven.                     |
| 🛏️  | **Mamá:** ¿Y tu hermana? ¿Ella **se levanta** sola?               | And your sister? Does she get up alone? |
| 🛏️  | **Hija 2:** ¡Sí! Yo **me levanto** sin ayuda.                     | Yes! I get up without help.             |
| 🥱  | **Papá:** Los fines de semana nosotros **nos despertamos** tarde. | On weekends we wake up late.            |

🔊 **Pronunciation focus:** des-pehr-**TAR**-seh (stress on TAR)

## Stem-Changing Reflexive Verbs (e→ie, o→ue, e→i) ⚠️

|     | Infinitivo         | Yo               | Tú                | Él/Ella          | Nosotros        | Vosotros 🇪🇸   | Ellos             |
| --- | ------------------ | ---------------- | ----------------- | ---------------- | --------------- | ------------- | ----------------- |
| ⏰  | despertarse (e→ie) | me despi**e**rto | te despi**e**rtas | se despi**e**rta | nos despertamos | os despertáis | se despi**e**rtan |
| 🛌  | acostarse (o→ue)   | me ac**ue**sto   | te ac**ue**stas   | se ac**ue**sta   | nos acostamos   | os acostáis   | se ac**ue**stan   |
| 💤  | dormirse (o→ue)    | me d**ue**rmo    | te d**ue**rmes    | se d**ue**rme    | nos dormimos    | os dormís     | se d**ue**rmen    |
| 👕  | vestirse (e→i)     | me v**i**sto     | te v**i**stes     | se v**i**ste     | nos vestimos    | os vestís     | se v**i**sten     |
| 🪑  | sentarse (e→ie)    | me s**ie**nto    | te s**ie**ntas    | se s**ie**nta    | nos sentamos    | os sentáis    | se s**ie**ntan    |
| 🤹  | divertirse (e→ie)  | me div**ie**rto  | te div**ie**rtes  | se div**ie**rte  | nos divertimos  | os divertís   | se div**ie**rten  |
| 👗  | probarse (o→ue)    | me pr**ue**bo    | te pr**ue**bas    | se pr**ue**ba    | nos probamos    | os probáis    | se pr**ue**ban    |

💡 **Pattern tip:** The stem ONLY changes when stressed! That's why nosotros/vosotros keep the original vowel.
