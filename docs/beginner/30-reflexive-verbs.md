# Reflexive Verbs — Verbos Reflexivos

**Level:** Beginner (A1)
**Estimated Time:** 10-15 minutes
**Topics:** Reflexive pronouns, daily routine verbs, reflexive verb conjugation

## Overview

Reflexive verbs describe actions you do to yourself — like waking up, showering, or getting dressed. They are essential for talking about your daily routine in Spanish. In this lesson you will learn the reflexive pronoun pattern, 10 core daily routine verbs, and how to conjugate them.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand what makes a verb reflexive (the action reflects back to the subject)
- Memorize the six reflexive pronouns (me, te, se, nos, os, se)
- Use 10 core reflexive verbs to describe a daily routine
- Conjugate a regular reflexive verb in all six persons

## Lesson Content

### What Are Reflexive Verbs?

Imagine looking in a mirror. When YOU do something to YOURSELF, that's reflexive!

```
Normal verb:    Yo lavo el coche.     → I wash the car.
Reflexive verb: Yo ME lavo.           → I wash MYSELF.
```

The action bounces back to you — the subject and the receiver are the same person. You spot reflexive verbs in the dictionary by the **-se** attached to the infinitive: lavarse, ducharse, vestirse.

### The Reflexive Pronoun Pattern

Every reflexive verb needs a pronoun that matches the subject. Here is the full pattern:

| Person        | Pronoun | Think of it as... |
| ------------- | ------- | ----------------- |
| Yo            | **me**  | "myself"          |
| Tú            | **te**  | "yourself"        |
| Él/Ella/Usted | **se**  | "himself/herself" |
| Nosotros      | **nos** | "ourselves"       |
| Vosotros 🇪🇸   | **os**  | "yourselves"      |
| Ellos/Ustedes | **se**  | "themselves"      |

> 💡 **Living in Spain tip:** You will hear "vosotros/os" every day in Valencia. In Latin America they use "ustedes" instead, but in Spain vosotros is standard.

The pronoun goes **before** the conjugated verb: _Yo **me** despierto._ With infinitives, it attaches to the end: _Voy a **ducharme**._

### Your 10 Core Daily Routine Verbs

These are the reflexive verbs you will use most often in everyday life:

| #   | Infinitivo  | Meaning            | Example                           |
| --- | ----------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| 1   | despertarse | to wake up         | Me despierto a las siete.         |
| 2   | levantarse  | to get up          | Me levanto después de la alarma.  |
| 3   | ducharse    | to shower          | Me ducho rápido por la mañana.    |
| 4   | lavarse     | to wash (oneself)  | Me lavo las manos antes de comer. |
| 5   | cepillarse  | to brush           | Me cepillo los dientes dos veces. |
| 6   | peinarse    | to comb one's hair | Me peino antes de salir.          |
| 7   | vestirse    | to get dressed     | Me visto en cinco minutos.        |
| 8   | sentarse    | to sit down        | Me siento en el sofá.             |
| 9   | acostarse   | to go to bed       | Me acuesto a las diez.            |
| 10  | dormirse    | to fall asleep     | Me duermo enseguida.              |

### Conjugation Example: lavarse (to wash oneself)

This is a regular reflexive verb. Notice how the pronoun changes with each person, but the verb conjugates normally:

| Person        | Conjugation |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| Yo            | me lavo     |
| Tú            | te lavas    |
| Él/Ella/Usted | se lava     |
| Nosotros      | nos lavamos |
| Vosotros 🇪🇸   | os laváis   |
| Ellos/Ustedes | se lavan    |

All the regular -ar reflexive verbs in your list (levantarse, ducharse, cepillarse, peinarse, sentarse) follow this same pattern — just swap the stem.

### Morning Routine Scenario

_La mañana — a family wakes up in Valencia_

|     | Español                                                           | English                                 |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| ⏰  | **Mamá:** ¡Buenos días! ¿A qué hora **te despiertas**?            | Good morning! What time do you wake up? |
| ⏰  | **Hija 1:** **Me despierto** a las siete.                         | I wake up at seven.                     |
| 🛏️  | **Mamá:** ¿Y tu hermana? ¿Ella **se levanta** sola?               | And your sister? Does she get up alone? |
| 🛏️  | **Hija 2:** ¡Sí! Yo **me levanto** sin ayuda.                     | Yes! I get up without help.             |
| 🥱  | **Papá:** Los fines de semana nosotros **nos despertamos** tarde. | On weekends we wake up late.            |

> **Your Turn:** Say what time you wake up: "Me despierto a las **\_\_**."

### Bathroom Routine Scenario

_El baño — getting ready for the day_

|     | Español                                                            | English                           |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| 🚿  | **Papá:** ¿Quién **se ducha** primero hoy?                         | Who showers first today?          |
| 🚿  | **Hija 1:** Yo **me ducho** rápido, ¡cinco minutos!                | I shower quickly, five minutes!   |
| 🧼  | **Mamá:** Recuerda **lavarte** las manos bien.                     | Remember to wash your hands well. |
| 🪥  | **Hija 2:** Siempre **me cepillo** los dientes dos veces.          | I always brush my teeth twice.    |
| 🪥  | **Mamá:** ¡Muy bien! Nosotros **nos cepillamos** después de comer. | Very good! We brush after eating. |

> **Your Turn:** Do you shower in the morning or at night? "Me ducho por la **\_\_** (mañana / noche)."

### Valencia Scenario: Walking the Turia

_Un paseo por el Turia — a family outing in Valencia's famous riverbed park_

|     | Español                                                 | English                                         |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 👟  | **Papá:** ¿**Os ponéis** las zapatillas cómodas?        | Are you putting on comfortable sneakers?        |
| 🌳  | **Mamá:** Hoy **nos relajamos** en el Turia.            | Today we are relaxing in the Turia.             |
| 🏃  | **Hija 1:** ¡Yo quiero correr! Nunca **me canso** aquí. | I want to run! I never get tired here.          |
| 🍦  | **Hija 2:** ¿**Nos sentamos** a tomar un helado?        | Shall we sit down to have an ice cream?         |
| 😎  | **Papá:** Claro. Después **nos quedamos** en el parque. | Of course. Afterwards we will stay in the park. |

> **Your Turn:** What do you do in the park? Try: "Me siento en un banco." (I sit on a bench.)

### Level 3: Getting Dressed

_Vestirse — getting ready to go out_

|     | Español                                            | English                                     |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 👕  | **Mamá:** Niñas, ¿**os vestís** solas hoy?         | Girls, are you getting dressed alone today? |
| 👗  | **Hija 1:** Sí, yo **me pongo** el vestido azul.   | Yes, I'm putting on the blue dress.         |
| 👟  | **Hija 2:** Yo **me pongo** las zapatillas nuevas. | I'm putting on my new sneakers.             |
| 🧥  | **Papá:** Hace frío. **Poneos** las chaquetas.     | It's cold. Put on your jackets.             |
| 👚  | **Mamá:** Yo **me visto** en cinco minutos.        | I get dressed in five minutes.              |

> **Grammar tip:** "Poneos" is the vosotros command form — you will hear this a LOT in Spain!

### Level 5: Bedtime Routine

_Hora de dormir — getting the family to sleep_

|     | Español                                                    | English                                      |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 🛌  | **Papá:** Venga, es hora de **acostarse**.                 | Come on, it's time to go to bed.             |
| 🩳  | **Hija 1:** Espera, todavía no **me he puesto** el pijama. | Wait, I haven't put on my pajamas yet.       |
| 🪥  | **Mamá:** ¿Ya **os habéis cepillado** los dientes?         | Have you already brushed your teeth?         |
| 💤  | **Hija 2:** Sí, pero no **me duermo** fácilmente.          | Yes, but I don't fall asleep easily.         |
| 📖  | **Papá:** Si **os acostáis** ya, os leo un cuento.         | If you go to bed now, I'll read you a story. |
| 😴  | **Mamá:** Ojalá todos **nos durmamos** pronto esta noche.  | Hopefully we all fall asleep soon tonight.   |

> **Pronunciation focus:** dor-**MEER**-seh (dormirse — stem changes o to ue: me d**ue**rmo)

|     | Español                                                | English                              |

| 💦  | **Mamá:** Recordad **hidratarse** bien.                | Remember to hydrate well.            |
| 😅  | **Hija 2:** Yo **me canso** en las subidas.            | I get tired on the climbs.           |
| 💪  | **Papá:** ¡Normal! Nosotros **nos estiramos** primero. | Normal! We stretch first.            |
| 🏔️  | **Mamá:** Al final todos **nos sentimos** orgullosos.  | In the end we all feel proud.        |

**New verbs:** abrigarse (to bundle up), cansarse (to get tired), estirarse (to stretch), hidratarse (to hydrate)

## Common Mistakes

| #   | Wrong                          | Right                               | Why                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Yo ducho por la mañana.        | Yo **me** ducho por la mañana.      | Reflexive verbs always need the pronoun.                              |
| 2   | Yo me lavo el coche.           | Yo lavo el coche.                   | Washing the car is not reflexive — the action is on the car, not you. |
| 3   | Ella me levanta a las ocho.    | Ella **se** levanta a las ocho.     | The pronoun must match the subject (ella = se).                       |
| 4   | Nosotros se despertamos tarde. | Nosotros **nos** despertamos tarde. | Nosotros always pairs with nos, never se.                             |
| 5   | Quiero me duchar.              | Quiero **ducharme**.                | With infinitives, the pronoun attaches to the end.                    |
