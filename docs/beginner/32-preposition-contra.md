## The Opposition: Against

---

## The Big Picture

### What Does CONTRA Mean?

**CONTRA = AGAINST**

Main uses:

- **AGAINST** (opposition)
- **AGAINST** (physical contact)
- **VERSUS** (competitions)

**💡 Think of it this way:** CONTRA = pushing against something

---

## 1️⃣ CONTRA for Opposition (AGAINST)

### What are you opposing? → CONTRA

**Use CONTRA for being against something**

### Examples

1. **Estoy contra esa idea**

   - (I'm against that idea)

2. **Luchamos contra el problema**

   - (We fight against the problem)

3. **Es una medicina contra el dolor**

   - (It's medicine against pain)

4. **Protesto contra la decisión**

   - (I protest against the decision)

5. **Voto contra esa ley**
   - (I vote against that law)

---

### Animal Examples - Natural Opposition

1. **El león lucha contra otros leones**

   - (The lion fights against other lions)

2. **Los animales se protegen contra el frío**

   - (Animals protect themselves against the cold)

3. **El perro ladra contra extraños**

   - (The dog barks against strangers)

4. **Las vacunas protegen contra enfermedades**
   - (Vaccines protect against illnesses)

## 2️⃣ CONTRA for Physical Contact (AGAINST)

### What is it touching? → CONTRA

**Use CONTRA when something touches or leans against something**

### Examples

1. **La bicicleta está contra la pared**

   - (The bike is against the wall)

2. **Me apoyo contra el árbol**

   - (I lean against the tree)

3. **El libro está contra la ventana**

   - (The book is against the window)

4. **Pone la escalera contra la casa**
   - (He puts the ladder against the house)

---

### Animal Examples

1. **El gato se frota contra mi pierna**

   - (The cat rubs against my leg)

2. **El perro se apoya contra la puerta**

   - (The dog leans against the door)

3. **El oso se rasca contra el árbol**

   - (The bear scratches against the tree)

4. **Las ovejas se amontonan contra la cerca**
   - (The sheep huddle against the fence)

## 3️⃣ CONTRA for Competition (VERSUS/AGAINST)

### Who's competing? → CONTRA

**Use CONTRA in sports and competitions**

### Examples

1. **España juega contra Italia**

   - (Spain plays against Italy)

2. **Mi equipo contra tu equipo**

   - (My team versus your team)

3. **La final es contra el campeón**

   - (The final is against the champion)

4. **Compito contra mi amigo**
   - (I compete against my friend)

---

### Sports Examples

1. **El partido es contra el mejor equipo**

   - (The match is against the best team)

2. **Juego contra jugadores mejores**

   - (I play against better players)

3. **La carrera es contra el tiempo**
   - (The race is against time)

---

## 4️⃣ Common CONTRA Phrases

### Must-Know Expressions

| Spanish               | English          | Example                     |
| --------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------- |
| en contra de          | against          | Estoy en contra de eso      |
| contra viento y marea | against all odds | Lucho contra viento y marea |
| chocar contra         | to crash against | Choca contra la pared       |
| ir contra             | to go against    | Va contra las reglas        |

---

### Examples

1. **El perro nada contra la corriente**

   - (The dog swims against the current)

2. **Voy contra las reglas a veces**

   - (I go against the rules sometimes)

3. **Los pájaros vuelan contra el viento**
   - (Birds fly against the wind)

## 5️⃣ Real-Life Scenarios

### Health

"Tomo vitaminas **contra** los resfriados. Es importante protegerse **contra** enfermedades. Lucho **contra** el cansancio cada día. Hago ejercicio como defensa **contra** el estrés. Los doctores trabajan **contra** las enfermedades."

_Translation: I take vitamins against colds. It's important to protect yourself against illnesses. I fight against tiredness every day. I exercise as a defense against stress. Doctors work against diseases._

---

### Sports

"Mañana jugamos **contra** el mejor equipo. Es una competición **contra** rivales fuertes. Compito **contra** mí mismo también. Lucho **contra** mis límites. **Contra** viento y marea, voy a ganar."
