## 1️⃣ Number Basics - Building Blocks

### 🌟 The Foundation: 0-10

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation | Memory Trick             |
| ------ | ------- | ------------- | ------------------------ |
| 0      | cero    | SEH-roh       | Like "zero" with accent  |
| 1      | uno     | OO-noh        | Say "uno!" when you win! |
| 2      | dos     | dohs          | Like "dose" of medicine  |
| 3      | tres    | trehs         | Three = tres             |
| 4      | cuatro  | KWAH-troh     | Sounds like "Quattro"    |
| 5      | cinco   | SEEN-koh      | Cinco de Mayo!           |
| 6      | seis    | sayss         | Sounds like "says"       |
| 7      | siete   | see-EH-teh    | Seven = siete (same S!)  |
| 8      | ocho    | OH-choh       | "Oh! CHO-colate!"        |
| 9      | nueve   | NWEH-veh      | "New" + "veh"            |
| 10     | diez    | dee-EHS       | "Diez" days = 10 days    |

**🎯 Practice Out Loud:**

- Count on your fingers: uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco...
- Count backwards: diez, nueve, ocho, siete...
- Skip count: dos, cuatro, seis, ocho, diez!

### 🌟 The Magic "Y" Rule: 21-99

**The Pattern:** tens + **y** + ones

| Number | Spanish          | Pattern                 |
| ------ | ---------------- | ----------------------- |
| 21     | veintiuno        | veinte + uno (special!) |
| 22     | veintidós        | veinte + dos (special!) |
| 23-29  | veinti...        | veinte + number         |
| 31     | treinta y uno    | treinta + Y + uno       |
| 45     | cuarenta y cinco | cuarenta + Y + cinco    |
| 68     | sesenta y ocho   | sesenta + Y + ocho      |
| 97     | noventa y siete  | noventa + Y + siete     |

**⚡ Important:**

- 21-29 use "veinti-" (one word, no spaces!)
- 31-99 use "y" between tens and ones

**Examples:**

- 24 = veinticuatro (one word!)
- 34 = treinta y cuatro (three words with Y!)
- 55 = cincuenta y cinco
- 78 = setenta y ocho

| 200    | doscientos    | dohs-see-EN-tohs      |

| 400    | cuatrocientos | kwah-troh-see-EN-tohs |

| 900    | novecientos   | noh-veh-see-EN-tohs   |

## 3️⃣ Number Patterns & Tricks

### 🎯 The "Y" Rule Explained

**When to use "Y" (and):**
✅ **YES - Use Y for 31-99:**

- 31 = treinta **y** uno
- 45 = cuarenta **y** cinco
- 98 = noventa **y** ocho

❌ **NO - Don't use Y:**

- 21-29 = veinti- (one word!)
  - 21 = veintiuno (NOT veinte y uno)
  - 27 = veintisiete (NOT veinte y siete)
- In hundreds
  - 125 = ciento veinticinco (NOT ciento y veinticinco)
- Between thousands and hundreds
  - 1,500 = mil quinientos (NOT mil y quinientos)

**✅ Correct:**

- 34 = treinta y cuatro
- 67 = sesenta y siete
- 235 = doscientos treinta y cinco (Y only between tens and ones!)

**❌ Wrong:**

- 25 = veinte y cinco ❌ (should be veinticinco)
- 100 = cien y ❌ (should be just cien)

---

### 🎯 Gender Agreement for 1 and 100s

**Spanish numbers agree in gender with nouns!**

**UNO/UNA (one):**

- un libro (one book - masculine)
- una casa (one house - feminine)
- veintiún años (21 years - masculine)
- veintiuna personas (21 people - feminine)

**CIENTOS/CIENTAS (hundreds):**

- doscientos euros (200 euros - masculine)
- doscientas personas (200 people - feminine)
- quinientos kilómetros (500 kilometers - masculine)
- quinientas calorías (500 calories - feminine)

**Examples:**

- Tengo un hermano = I have one brother
- Tengo una hermana = I have one sister
- Hay treinta y un días en enero = There are 31 days in January
- Hay treinta y una personas = There are 31 people
- Doscientos hombres = 200 men
- Doscientas mujeres = 200 women

---

### 🎯 Common Mistakes to Avoid

| ❌ Wrong      | ✅ Correct   | Why                        |
| ------------- | ------------ | -------------------------- |
| Uno mil       | Mil          | Never say "uno" before mil |
| Diez y seis   | Dieciséis    | 16-19 are one word         |
| Veinte y tres | Veintitrés   | 21-29 are one word         |
| Ciento        | Cien         | Use "cien" for exactly 100 |
| Cien cinco    | Ciento cinco | Use "ciento" for 101-199   |
