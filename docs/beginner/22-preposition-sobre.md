# 📚 Preposition Puzzle v10 - SOBRE

## The Topic Master: About/On/Over

**⏱️ Study Time: 10-12 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Master SOBRE - for topics, physical position, and approximations!**

---

## The Big Picture

### What Does SOBRE Mean?

**SOBRE has three main uses:**

1. **ABOUT** (topic/subject)

   - Hablo sobre animales (I talk about animals)

2. **ON/ON TOP OF** (physical position)

   - El libro está sobre la mesa (The book is on the table)

3. **ABOUT/AROUND** (approximate time/quantity)
   - Sobre las tres (Around 3 o'clock)

**💡 Think of it this way:** SOBRE = on top of (literally or figuratively)

---

## 1️⃣ SOBRE for Topics (ABOUT)

### What's the topic? → SOBRE

**Use SOBRE when talking ABOUT something**

### Examples

1. **Hablo sobre animales**

   - (I talk about animals)

2. **Es un libro sobre perros**

   - (It's a book about dogs)

3. **La película es sobre leones**

   - (The movie is about lions)

4. **Escribo sobre mi familia**

   - (I write about my family)

5. **Una conversación sobre el clima**
   - (A conversation about the weather)

---

### 🐘 Animal Topic Examples

1. **Leo un artículo sobre elefantes**

   - (I read an article about elephants)

2. **Veo un documental sobre delfines**

   - (I watch a documentary about dolphins)

3. **Aprendo sobre las ballenas**

   - (I learn about whales)

4. **Este programa es sobre animales salvajes**

   - (This program is about wild animals)

5. **Hablamos sobre nuestras mascotas**
   - (We talk about our pets)

---

### 💡 SOBRE vs DE for Topics

**Both can mean "about" but slight difference:**

**SOBRE** = about (more formal, general topic)

- Un libro **sobre** historia (A book about history - general subject)

**DE** = about/of (more specific, possession of topic)

- Un libro **de** animales (An animal book - type of book)

**Often interchangeable, but:**

- Hablo **sobre** política (I talk about politics - discussing the topic)
- Hablo **de** mi perro (I talk about my dog - specific thing)

---

### ✏️ Practice: SOBRE for Topics

1. Hablo **\_\_\_** mi familia
2. Es un libro **\_\_\_** gatos
3. La película es **\_\_\_** dinosaurios
4. Escribo **\_\_\_** mi vida
5. Aprendo **\_\_\_** España

**Answers:** All answers are "sobre"!

## 2️⃣ SOBRE for Physical Position (ON/ON TOP OF)

### Where is it physically? → SOBRE

**Use SOBRE when something is ON TOP OF something else**

### Examples

1. **El libro está sobre la mesa**

   - (The book is on the table)

2. **El gato está sobre el sofá**

   - (The cat is on the sofa)

3. **Las llaves están sobre el escritorio**

   - (The keys are on the desk)

4. **El pájaro está sobre la rama**

   - (The bird is on the branch)

5. **La lámpara está sobre la mesa**
   - (The lamp is on the table)

---

### 🐦 Animal Position Examples

1. **El pájaro se posa sobre el árbol**

   - (The bird perches on the tree)

2. **La mariposa se posa sobre la flor**

   - (The butterfly lands on the flower)

3. **El gato salta sobre la caja**

   - (The cat jumps onto the box)

4. **El loro está sobre su percha**
   - (The parrot is on its perch)

---

### 💡 SOBRE vs EN for "On"

**Both can mean "on" but different nuance:**

**SOBRE** = on top of (emphasis on being above)

- El libro está **sobre** la mesa (on top of the table - above it)

**EN** = on (general position, part of)

- El libro está **en** la mesa (on the table - resting on it)

**Often interchangeable for "on"!**

- Both are correct for most situations
- SOBRE emphasizes "on top of"
- EN is more neutral

---

### ✏️ Practice: SOBRE for Position

1. El vaso está **\_\_\_** la mesa
2. El gato duerme **\_\_\_** la cama
3. Las llaves están **\_\_\_** el escritorio
4. El pájaro vuela **\_\_\_** el árbol (over/above)
5. La foto está **\_\_\_** la pared (on the wall)

**Answers:** All answers are "sobre"!

## 3️⃣ SOBRE for Approximation (ABOUT/AROUND)

### Approximately what time/amount? → SOBRE

**Use SOBRE for approximate times or quantities**

### Examples - Time

1. **Llego sobre las tres**

   - (I arrive around 3 o'clock)

2. **Vengo sobre las cinco**

   - (I come around 5)

3. **La reunión es sobre el mediodía**
   - (The meeting is around noon)

---

### Examples - Quantity

4. **Hay sobre veinte personas**

   - (There are about 20 people)

5. **Cuesta sobre diez euros**

   - (It costs around 10 euros)

6. **Hay sobre cien animales en el zoo**
   - (There are about 100 animals in the zoo)

---

### 💡 SOBRE vs ALREDEDOR DE

**Both mean "around/about" for approximation:**

**SOBRE** = about/around (common with time)

- Llego **sobre** las tres (I arrive around 3)

**ALREDEDOR DE** = around (more common with quantity)

- **Alrededor de** veinte personas (Around 20 people)

**Both are correct! SOBRE is simpler.**

---

### ✏️ Practice: SOBRE for Approximation

1. Llego **\_\_\_** las cuatro (around)
2. Hay **\_\_\_** cincuenta estudiantes (about)
3. Cuesta **\_\_\_** quince euros (around)
4. Vienen **\_\_\_** las dos (around)
5. Hay **\_\_\_** diez perros en el parque (about)

**Answers:** All answers are "sobre"!

## 4️⃣ Common SOBRE Phrases

### Must-Know Expressions

| Spanish         | English              | Example                 |
| --------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| sobre todo      | especially/above all | Sobre todo, los perros  |
| sobre eso       | about that           | Hablamos sobre eso      |
| sobre la marcha | on the go/improvised | Lo hago sobre la marcha |

---

### 🐕 Examples

1. **Me gustan los animales, sobre todo los perros**

   - (I like animals, especially dogs)

2. **Hablamos sobre eso ayer**

   - (We talked about that yesterday)

3. **El pájaro construye su nido sobre la marcha**
   - (The bird builds its nest on the go)

## 5️⃣ Real-Life Scenarios

### 📖 Reading Time

"Leo un libro **sobre** animales marinos. Está **sobre** mi mesita de noche. Aprendo **sobre** delfines y ballenas. **Sobre todo**, me interesan los tiburones. El libro tiene **sobre** trescientas páginas. Leo **sobre** las ocho de la noche. Es fascinante."

_Translation: I read a book about marine animals. It's on my nightstand. I learn about dolphins and whales. Especially, I'm interested in sharks. The book has about 300 pages. I read around 8 at night. It's fascinating._

---

### 🐾 At the Vet

"Hablo con el veterinario **sobre** mi gato. El gato está **sobre** la mesa de examen. Pregunto **sobre** su salud. La cita cuesta **sobre** cincuenta euros. Llegamos **sobre** las tres. **Sobre todo**, quiero saber si está sano."
