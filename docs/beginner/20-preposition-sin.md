# 🚫 Preposition Puzzle v8 - SIN

## The Opposite: Without

**⏱️ Study Time: 8-10 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Master SIN - the opposite of CON!**

---

## The Big Picture

### What Does SIN Mean?

**SIN = WITHOUT (simple!)**

If CON means "with," then SIN means "without"!

**Formula:** SIN + noun/pronoun/infinitive

---

## 1️⃣ SIN for Absence (WITHOUT something)

### What's missing? → SIN

**Use SIN to show absence or lack**

### Examples

1. **Café sin azúcar**

   - (Coffee without sugar)

2. **Pan sin sal**

   - (Bread without salt)

3. **Una casa sin jardín**

   - (A house without a garden)

4. **Agua sin gas**

   - (Water without gas / still water)

5. **Leche sin lactosa**
   - (Milk without lactose)

---

### 🍽️ Food Examples

1. **Té sin azúcar**

   - (Tea without sugar)

2. **Pizza sin queso**

   - (Pizza without cheese)

3. **Comida sin gluten**

   - (Food without gluten)

4. **Café sin leche**
   - (Coffee without milk)

---

### 🐾 Animal Examples

1. **Un perro sin cola**

   - (A dog without a tail)

2. **Un gato sin pelo**

   - (A cat without fur / hairless cat)

3. **Un pájaro sin plumas**

   - (A bird without feathers)

4. **Un pez sin agua**
   - (A fish without water)

---

### ✏️ Practice: SIN for Absence

1. Café **\_\_\_** leche
2. Pan **\_\_\_** mantequilla
3. Una casa **\_\_\_** ventanas
4. Agua **\_\_\_** gas
5. Un perro **\_\_\_** dueño (owner)

**Answers:** All answers are "sin"!

---

## 2️⃣ SIN + Pronoun

### Without ME, YOU, etc.

**SIN + pronoun (doesn't change like CON!)**

**Forms:**

- sin mí (without me)
- sin ti (without you)
- sin él (without him)
- sin ella (without her)
- sin nosotros (without us)
- sin ellos (without them)

**⚠️ Note:** Unlike CON (conmigo/contigo), SIN keeps the normal forms!

### Examples

1. **No voy sin ti**

   - (I'm not going without you)

2. **Sin mí, no pueden hacerlo**

   - (Without me, they can't do it)

3. **El perro está triste sin su dueño**

   - (The dog is sad without its owner)

4. **No puedo vivir sin ella**
   - (I can't live without her)

---

### 🐕 Examples

1. **Mi perro no sale sin mí**

   - (My dog doesn't go out without me)

2. **El gatito llora sin su madre**

   - (The kitten cries without its mother)

3. **Sin ti, no es divertido**
   - (Without you, it's not fun)

---

### ✏️ Practice: SIN + Pronouns

1. No voy **\_\_\_** ti (without you)
2. **\_\_\_** mí, no funciona (without me)
3. El perro no come **\_\_\_** su comida favorita (without)
4. **\_\_\_** ellos, no podemos jugar (without them)

**Answers:** 1. sin 2. Sin 3. sin 4. Sin

---

## 3️⃣ SIN + Infinitive (WITHOUT doing)

### Without doing what? → SIN + verb

**Use SIN + infinitive for actions not done**

### Examples

1. **Salgo sin decir adiós**

   - (I leave without saying goodbye)

2. **Come sin masticar**

   - (He eats without chewing)

3. **Voy sin pensar**

   - (I go without thinking)

4. **Habla sin parar**

   - (He speaks without stopping)

5. **Leo sin entender**
   - (I read without understanding)

---

### 🐾 Animal Examples

1. **El gato entra sin hacer ruido**

   - (The cat enters without making noise)

2. **El perro come sin masticar**

   - (The dog eats without chewing)

3. **Los pájaros vuelan sin cansarse**

   - (Birds fly without getting tired)

4. **El pez nada sin parar**
   - (The fish swims without stopping)

---

### ✏️ Practice: SIN + Infinitive

1. Salgo **\_\_\_** decir nada (without saying)
2. Come **\_\_\_** pensar (without thinking)
3. El gato entra **\_\_\_** hacer ruido (without making)
4. Trabajo **\_\_\_** descansar (without resting)
5. Habla **\_\_\_** parar (without stopping)

**Answers:** All answers are "sin"!

## 4️⃣ Common SIN Phrases

### Must-Know Expressions

| Spanish      | English       | Example                 |
| ------------ | ------------- | ----------------------- |
| sin duda     | without doubt | Sin duda, es cierto     |
| sin embargo  | however       | Sin embargo, es difícil |
| sin problema | no problem    | Sin problema, te ayudo  |
| sin falta    | without fail  | Llego sin falta         |
| sin parar    | non-stop      | Trabaja sin parar       |
| sin rumbo    | aimlessly     | Camina sin rumbo        |

---

### 🐦 Examples

1. **Sin duda, los perros son leales**

   - (Without doubt, dogs are loyal)

2. **Sin embargo, los gatos son independientes**

   - (However, cats are independent)

3. **El pájaro canta sin parar**
   - (The bird sings non-stop)

## 5️⃣ CON vs SIN - Opposites

### Side-by-Side

| CON (With)     | SIN (Without)     |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| con azúcar     | sin azúcar        |
| (with sugar)   | (without sugar)   |
| con leche      | sin leche         |
| (with milk)    | (without milk)    |
| con amigos     | sin amigos        |
| (with friends) | (without friends) |
| con dinero     | sin dinero        |
| (with money)   | (without money)   |

---

### ✏️ Practice: CON or SIN?

1. Café **\_\_\_** azúcar (without)
2. Voy **\_\_\_** mi hermano (with)
3. Pan **\_\_\_** sal (without)
4. Escribo **\_\_\_** bolígrafo (with)
5. Una casa **\_\_\_** jardín (without)
6. Como **\_\_\_** tenedor (with)

**Answers:** 1. sin 2. con 3. sin 4. con 5. sin 6. con

## 6️⃣ Real-Life Scenarios

### 🍽️ Ordering Food

"Quiero un café **sin** azúcar y **con** leche. También pan **sin** gluten, por favor. Mi amigo quiere té **sin** leche pero **con** miel. Él no puede comer **sin** miel - le encanta. Comemos **sin** prisa. **Sin** duda, este lugar es excelente."

_Translation: I want a coffee without sugar and with milk. Also gluten-free bread, please. My friend wants tea without milk but with honey. He can't eat without honey - he loves it. We eat without rushing. Without doubt, this place is excellent._

---

### 🐕 At the Park

"Voy al parque **con** mi perro. Nunca voy **sin** él. Él no puede estar **sin** mí. Jugamos **sin** parar. Otros perros juegan **con** nosotros. **Sin** embargo, mi perro es el más rápido. Volvemos a casa **sin** problemas."
