## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐⭐ (Core Exam Knowledge)

## 🎯 What This Topic Covers

Knowing **which lights to use and when** is a frequent DGT exam topic. Spain has specific rules for daytime, night-time, tunnels, fog, rain, and motorway driving. Using the wrong lights — or no lights — is an infraction carrying fines up to €200.

## 💡 THE 6 TYPES OF VEHICLE LIGHTS

| Light Type                                | Spanish                            | Colour                | When Used                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sidelights / Position**                 | Luces de posición                  | White front, red rear | Always on with any other light. Also when parked at night on poorly lit roads             |
| **Dipped beam** (US: low beam)            | Luces de cruce (corto alcance)     | White                 | THE most important light — night, tunnels, fog, rain, reduced visibility                  |
| **Main beam / Full beam** (US: high beam) | Luces de carretera (largo alcance) | White                 | Night on interurban roads when NO oncoming traffic. Switch to dipped when traffic appears |
| **Front fog lights**                      | Luces antiniebla delanteras        | White or yellow       | Fog, heavy rain, snow, dust clouds — can be used with or instead of dipped beam           |
| **Rear fog light**                        | Luz antiniebla trasera             | Red                   | ONLY in very poor visibility (dense fog, heavy rain). Turn OFF when conditions improve    |
| **Hazard lights**                         | Luces de emergencia                | Amber flashing        | Breakdown, accident, sudden obstacle, or significantly below normal speed                 |

## 🌙 WHEN TO USE WHICH LIGHTS

### Night-time — De Noche (Between Sunset and Sunrise)

| Situation                                     | Lights Required                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Interurban road, no oncoming traffic**      | Full beam (largo alcance) + position lights                               |
| **Interurban road, oncoming traffic visible** | Dipped beam (cruce) — switch from full to dipped!                         |
| **Urban road (street-lit)**                   | Dipped beam (cruce) only — full beam would dazzle                         |
| **Urban road (poorly lit)**                   | Dipped beam; full beam only if road is truly dark and no oncoming traffic |
| **Tunnel**                                    | Dipped beam (cruce) — ALWAYS, even if tunnel is well lit                  |
| **Parked on poorly lit road**                 | Position lights (posición) at minimum                                     |

### Reduced Visibility — Visibilidad Reducida

| Condition             | Lights Required                                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🌫️ **Fog**            | Dipped beam + front fog lights. Rear fog light in dense fog. NEVER use full beam in fog (it reflects back and blinds you!) |
| 🌧️ **Heavy rain**     | Dipped beam. Front fog lights optional. Rear fog if very heavy                                                             |
| ❄️ **Snow**           | Dipped beam + front fog lights                                                                                             |
| 🌬️ **Dust/sandstorm** | Dipped beam + front fog lights                                                                                             |
| 🌅 **Dawn/dusk**      | Dipped beam                                                                                                                |

### Daytime — De Día

| Situation                          | Lights Required                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Normal conditions, urban**       | DRLs (automatic on modern cars) or no lights required                            |
| **Normal conditions, interurban**  | DRLs recommended but not strictly mandatory for cars (mandatory for motorcycles) |
| **Tunnel (even short ones!)**      | Dipped beam — COMPULSORY                                                         |
| **Reduced visibility (rain, fog)** | Dipped beam — COMPULSORY                                                         |
| **Motorcycles**                    | Dipped beam ALWAYS — day and night                                               |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Traps — Lighting

1. **FULL BEAM IN FOG = WRONG.** This is the #1 lighting exam trap. Full beam reflects off fog and blinds you. Use dipped beam + fog lights instead.
2. **Rear fog light: turn it OFF** when conditions improve. Leaving it on dazzles the driver behind you. Fine: €200.
3. **Tunnels: ALWAYS use dipped beam**, even during the day, even if the tunnel is well lit.
4. **Flash your lights to warn:** You may flash your headlights to warn of a hazard — but NOT to warn of police checkpoints (that's illegal and carries a fine).
5. **Full beam when parked = prohibited.** You must use position lights only, or dipped beam at most.

## 🔦 SPECIAL LIGHTING SITUATIONS

### Overtaking at Night

When overtaking at night on an interurban road:

1. Flash your full beam briefly to alert the vehicle ahead
2. Switch to dipped beam as you pull alongside (to avoid dazzling through their mirrors)
3. Once past and clear, you may return to full beam

### Being Dazzled

If an oncoming vehicle dazzles you with full beam:

1. **Look to the RIGHT edge** of the road (not at the light!)
2. Reduce speed
3. Flash your lights once to signal them to dip — but do NOT retaliate with your own full beam

### Breakdown on the Road (2026)

When stopped due to breakdown or accident:

1. Turn on **hazard lights** immediately
2. Activate your **V-16 beacon** (place on roof)
3. Keep position lights on
4. Do NOT leave the vehicle unless absolutely necessary

## 🗣️ Conjugation Spotlight

### ENCENDER — to turn on / to switch on (lights)

A stem-changing verb: **e → ie** in the present.

|             | Presente   | Pretérito    | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | ---------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| yo          | enciendo   | encendí      | encendía    |
| tú          | enciendes  | encendiste   | encendías   |
| él/ella     | enciende   | encendió     | encendía    |
| nosotros    | encendemos | encendimos   | encendíamos |
| vosotros    | encendéis  | encendisteis | encendíais  |
| ellos/ellas | encienden  | encendieron  | encendían   |

**Key sentences:**

- _Enciendo las luces de cruce al entrar en el túnel._ — I turn on dipped headlights when entering the tunnel.
- _Encienda las luces antiniebla, hay mucha niebla._ — Turn on the fog lights, it's very foggy.

### APAGAR — to turn off / to switch off

|             | Presente | Pretérito  | Imperfecto |
| ----------- | -------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| yo          | apago    | apagué     | apagaba    |
| tú          | apagas   | apagaste   | apagabas   |
| él/ella     | apaga    | apagó      | apagaba    |
| nosotros    | apagamos | apagamos   | apagábamos |
| vosotros    | apagáis  | apagasteis | apagabais  |
| ellos/ellas | apagan   | apagaron   | apagaban   |

**Key sentences:**

- _Apague la luz antiniebla trasera cuando mejore la visibilidad._ — Turn off the rear fog light when visibility improves.
- _Apagué las luces de carretera al ver tráfico de frente._ — I switched off full beam when I saw oncoming traffic.

## 🏙️ Valencia Connection

- **Tunnels:** The tunnels on the V-30 and approaching the port — always dipped beam
- **Fog:** Rare in Valencia city but common on morning drives toward the Albufera or inland mountains
- **Rain:** Valencia experiences heavy Mediterranean downpours (gota fría) — dipped beam essential
- **Urban driving at night:** Av. de Francia and Av. de las Balears are well lit — dipped beam only, never full beam
- **Parking at night:** Position lights in darker residential streets of Penya-Roja
- **Motorcycles:** Many motorcycles in Valencia — they always have dipped beam on, day and night

## ⚠️ US → Spain Differences

| US                            | Spain                                                                         | Key Difference                            |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| "Headlights" used generically | Spain distinguishes **cruce** (dipped) from **carretera** (full) precisely    | You must know the difference for the exam |
| DRLs vary by state/vehicle    | DRLs not mandatory for cars but recommended. Mandatory for motorcycles        | Spain is less strict on car DRLs          |
| Fog lights: driver's choice   | Front fog: optional in bad conditions. **Rear fog: MUST turn off** when clear | Leaving rear fog on = fine                |
| Flash to warn: informal       | Flash to warn of hazard: OK. Flash to warn of police: **ILLEGAL**             | €200 fine for warning of police           |
| Main-beam etiquette: courtesy | Main-beam rules: **legal obligation** to switch to dipped                     | Spain is stricter                         |
| Tunnels: varies               | Tunnels: dipped beam **ALWAYS compulsory**                                    | Even short, well-lit tunnels              |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **Dipped beam (luces de cruce) is the default for almost every situation** — night, rain, fog, tunnels.
2. **Full beam (luces de carretera): interurban roads only**, when NO oncoming traffic. Switch to dipped immediately when you see lights.
3. **NEVER use full beam in fog** — it reflects back and blinds you. Use dipped + fog lights.
4. **Tunnels = dipped beam ALWAYS.** Even in daytime, even if well lit.
5. **Rear fog light: turn it OFF** when conditions improve. It dazzles drivers behind you.
6. **Motorcycles must use dipped beam 24/7** — day and night.
7. **Flashing to warn of police checkpoints is ILLEGAL** — €200 fine.
8. **If dazzled: look RIGHT, slow down, flash once** — never retaliate with full beam.
9. **V-16 beacon (2026) + hazard lights** for breakdowns — don't leave the vehicle.
10. **Key verbs:** _encender_ (to turn on), _apagar_ (to turn off), _deslumbrar_ (to dazzle).
