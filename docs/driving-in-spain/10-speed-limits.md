# 10 — Speed Limits / Límites de Velocidad

## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐⭐ (Core Exam Knowledge)

## 🎯 What This Topic Covers

Speed limits in Spain are set by road type, vehicle type, and conditions. This is one of the most **number-heavy** DGT topics — you need to memorize specific figures. The good news: the system is logical once you see the pattern. Urban limits changed significantly in 2021, and new targeted reductions on motorway sections are being introduced in 2026.

## 🚗 THE MASTER SPEED TABLE — For Cars (Turismo, Permiso B)

### Urban Roads — Dentro de Poblado

| Road Type                    | Limit       | Spanish Rule                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 lane per direction**     | **30 km/h** | Calle de un carril por sentido                        |
| **2+ lanes per direction**   | **50 km/h** | Calle de dos o más carriles por sentido               |
| **Residential zone (S-220)** | **20 km/h** | Zona residencial — pedestrians have absolute priority |
| **Zone 30 (S-28)**           | **30 km/h** | Zona 30 — shared space, calmed traffic                |

**💡 The 2021 Urban Change:** Before 2021, ALL urban roads defaulted to 50 km/h. Now, single-lane streets default to 30 km/h. This is a very common DGT exam question!

### Interurban Roads — Fuera de Poblado

| Road Type                                     | Limit (Cars)           | Spanish Name                              |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Autopista** (motorway, may be tolled)       | **120 km/h**           | Autopista                                 |
| **Autovía** (dual carriageway, free)          | **120 km/h**           | Autovía                                   |
| **Conventional road, 2+ lanes per direction** | **90 km/h**            | Carretera convencional (2+ carriles)      |
| **Conventional road, 1 lane per direction**   | **90 km/h**            | Carretera convencional (1 carril)         |
| **Road for motor vehicles (S-3)**             | **100 km/h**           | Vía para automóviles                      |
| **Travesía** (interurban road through a town) | **Urban limits apply** | Travesía — follows the town's speed rules |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — Conventional Roads

Before 2019, conventional roads with one lane per direction had a limit of 100 km/h. It was **reduced to 90 km/h** in January 2019. The DGT still tests whether you know the current limit.

## 🚛 SPEED LIMITS BY VEHICLE TYPE — Fuera de Poblado

| Vehicle               | Autopista/Autovía | Conventional Road   |
| --------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| 🚗 Car (turismo)      | 120 km/h          | 90 km/h             |
| 🏍️ Motorcycle         | 120 km/h          | 90 km/h             |
| 🚐 Car towing trailer | 80 km/h           | 70 km/h             |
| 🚌 Bus/Coach          | 100 km/h          | 90 km/h             |
| 🚛 Lorry >3,500 kg    | 90 km/h           | 80 km/h             |
| 🛵 Moped (ciclomotor) | ❌ Not allowed    | 45 km/h (by design) |

**Special rule:** School buses and vehicles carrying dangerous goods must reduce their interurban speed by **10 km/h** below the normal limit.

## ⬇️ MINIMUM SPEED — Velocidad Mínima

Driving too slowly is also dangerous and illegal in Spain!

| Road Type               | Minimum Speed               |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Autopista / Autovía** | **60 km/h**                 |
| **Other roads**         | **Half the posted maximum** |

**Example:** On a road with an 80 km/h limit, the minimum is 40 km/h.

**Exception:** You may drive below the minimum when conditions demand it (heavy rain, fog, traffic jam, breakdown), but you should use hazard lights if significantly below the normal speed.

**💡 Mick's Memory Trick:** "60 on the motorway, half everywhere else."

---

## 🌧️ WHEN TO REDUCE SPEED — Cuándo Reducir la Velocidad

The posted speed limit is the MAXIMUM in **ideal conditions**. You must drive slower when:

| Condition           | Spanish          | Action                                           |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 🌧️ Rain             | Lluvia           | Reduce speed, increase following distance        |
| 🌫️ Fog              | Niebla           | Reduce significantly, use fog lights             |
| ❄️ Ice/Snow         | Hielo/Nieve      | Reduce significantly, consider chains            |
| 🌬️ Strong wind      | Viento fuerte    | Reduce, grip steering firmly                     |
| 🌙 Night            | Noche            | Drive only as fast as your headlights illuminate |
| 🚧 Roadworks        | Obras            | Obey temporary speed signs                       |
| 🏫 Near schools     | Zona escolar     | Extra caution, expect children                   |
| 🚶 Pedestrian areas | Zonas peatonales | Maximum caution                                  |

**Key DGT concept: "Velocidad adecuada"** — You must always drive at a speed appropriate for conditions, even if it's well below the posted limit. Driving at the limit in heavy rain could be considered reckless.

## 💰 SPEEDING FINES & POINTS — Multas y Pérdida de Puntos

| Excess Over Limit | Fine | Points Lost |
| ----------------- | ---- | ----------- |
| 1–20 km/h over    | €100 | 0 points    |
| 21–30 km/h over   | €300 | 2 points    |
| 31–40 km/h over   | €400 | 4 points    |
| 41–50 km/h over   | €500 | 6 points    |
| 50+ km/h over     | €600 | 6 points    |

**Remember:** New drivers start with **8 points** (experienced drivers can have up to 15). Losing 8 points = licence suspended.

**Speed cameras (radares):** Spain has an extensive network of fixed and mobile speed cameras, especially on the V-30 around Valencia, the AP-7, and urban approaches. The miDGT app shows some fixed camera locations.

## 🗣️ Conjugation Spotlight

### REDUCIR — to reduce

An irregular verb: **c → zc** in the yo present (like _conducir_).

|             | Presente  | Pretérito   | Imperfecto |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------- | ---------- |
| yo          | reduzco   | reduje      | reducía    |
| tú          | reduces   | redujiste   | reducías   |
| él/ella     | reduce    | redujo      | reducía    |
| nosotros    | reducimos | redujimos   | reducíamos |
| vosotros    | reducís   | redujisteis | reducíais  |
| ellos/ellas | reducen   | redujeron   | reducían   |

**Note:** The preterite is highly irregular: _reduje, redujiste, redujo..._ (like _conducir → conduje_).

**Key sentences:**

- _Reduzco la velocidad en zonas de obras._ — I reduce speed in roadworks zones.
- _Reduje la velocidad porque llovía._ — I reduced speed because it was raining.
- _Es obligatorio reducir la velocidad cerca de colegios._ — It is compulsory to reduce speed near schools.

### CONDUCIR — to drive

|             | Presente   | Pretérito    | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | ---------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| yo          | conduzco   | conduje      | conducía    |
| tú          | conduces   | condujiste   | conducías   |
| él/ella     | conduce    | condujo      | conducía    |
| nosotros    | conducimos | condujimos   | conducíamos |
| vosotros    | conducís   | condujisteis | conducíais  |
| ellos/ellas | conducen   | condujeron   | conducían   |

**Key sentences:**

- _Conduzco a 120 km/h por la autovía._ — I drive at 120 km/h on the dual carriageway.
- _Ayer conduje por la V-30._ — Yesterday I drove on the V-30.

## 🏙️ Valencia Connection

- **Zona 30:** Many streets in Camins al Grau, Penya-Roja, and around the Turia are 30 km/h zones
- **50 km/h:** Major multi-lane avenues like Av. de las Balears, Av. del Puerto, and Av. de Francia
- **V-30 ring road:** 120 km/h limit but frequently reduced to 80–100 km/h by variable signs during rush hour
- **AP-7:** 120 km/h on the coastal motorway — watch for the 2026 sections reduced to 100 km/h
- **N-332 travesías:** When the N-332 passes through small towns south toward Cullera, urban limits apply
- **Speed cameras:** Fixed radars on the V-30 approaches and the Av. del Puerto entering the city
- **ZBE overlap:** Speed limits in Valencia's growing low emission zone are generally 30 km/h

## ⚠️ US → Spain Differences for Speed Limits

| US                                          | Spain                                                       | Key Difference                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Speed in mph                                | Speed in **km/h**                                           | 120 km/h ≈ 75 mph. 30 km/h ≈ 19 mph                                        |
| School zones ~25 mph (40 km/h)              | Near schools: no fixed generic limit, use warning sign P-21 | Spain relies on Zona 30 and warning signs, not flashing school zone lights |
| Speed limits on rectangular white signs     | Speed limits on **round red-bordered** signs (R-301)        | Different shape!                                                           |
| Residential area ~25 mph                    | Residential zone (S-220) = **20 km/h**                      | Much lower in Spain                                                        |
| Interstate ~65–75 mph                       | Autopista/autovía = 120 km/h (~75 mph)                      | Similar top speed                                                          |
| Rural road ~55 mph (89 km/h)                | Conventional road = **90 km/h** (~56 mph)                   | Almost identical!                                                          |
| No minimum speed on most roads              | Minimum 60 km/h on motorways, half-limit elsewhere          | Spain enforces minimums                                                    |
| Points accumulate as penalties              | Points START at 8 and you LOSE them                         | Reversed system                                                            |
| Speed cameras less common (varies by state) | Speed cameras VERY common, fixed + mobile                   | Spain has one of the densest radar networks in Europe                      |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **Urban 1-lane streets = 30 km/h. Urban 2+ lanes = 50 km/h.** (Changed in 2021!)
2. **Residential zone (S-220) = 20 km/h.** Pedestrians have absolute priority.
3. **Autopista/autovía = 120 km/h. Conventional road = 90 km/h.**
4. **Minimum speed on motorway = 60 km/h.** Other roads = half the maximum.
5. **Car towing trailer: 80 km/h motorway, 70 km/h conventional.**
6. **Always reduce speed** for rain, fog, night, roadworks, schools.
7. **"Velocidad adecuada"** = the speed appropriate for conditions, even if below the limit.
8. **Speeding fines start at €100** and can reach €600 with 6 points lost.
9. **New drivers start with 8 points** — losing all of them suspends your licence.
10. **Key verbs:** _reducir_ (to reduce), _conducir_ (to drive), _respetar_ (to respect), _superar_ (to exceed).
