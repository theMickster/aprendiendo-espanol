### 💡 How to Choose

When visiting autoescuelas, ask these questions (in Spanish — great practice!):

| Question in Spanish                                   | English                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| _¿Cuánto cuesta el curso completo para el permiso B?_ | How much does the full B licence course cost?  |
| _¿Tienen profesores que hablan inglés?_               | Do you have instructors who speak English?     |
| _¿Puedo hacer el examen teórico en inglés?_           | Can I take the theory exam in English?         |
| _¿Cuántas clases prácticas necesito normalmente?_     | How many practical lessons do I normally need? |
| _¿Qué tasa de aprobados tienen a la primera?_         | What's your first-time pass rate?              |
| _¿Tienen coche automático o solo manual?_             | Do you have automatic or only manual?          |
| _¿Cuánto tardan en darme fecha de examen?_            | How long until you can give me an exam date?   |

## 📋 The Process Checklist — Your Step-by-Step

Use this checklist to track your progress:

- [ ] **Choose an autoescuela** — visit 2–3, compare prices and feeling
- [ ] **Enrol** (matricularse) — pay the initial fee
- [ ] **Pass the psicotécnico** — medical exam at a CRC (centro de reconocimiento)
- [ ] **Study theory** — use PracticaTest/TraficoTest + this wiki
- [ ] **Pass the theory exam** — 30 questions, max 3 errors, request English
- [ ] **Take practical lessons** — average ~20 lessons, your instructor decides when you're ready
- [ ] **Buy V-16 beacon** — DGT-approved, connected model (check dgt.es/v16 list)
- [ ] **Pass the practical exam** — ~25 minutes, in Spanish, in autoescuela's car
- [ ] **Receive provisional licence** — valid 3 months, from your autoescuela
- [ ] **Receive final carnet** — arrives by post, ~6 weeks
- [ ] **Display green L for 1 year** — new driver requirement
