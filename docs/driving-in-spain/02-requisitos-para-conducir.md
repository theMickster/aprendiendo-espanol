## 📋 The Requirements — What You Need to Know

### Who Can Get a Permiso B?

To obtain a standard Class B driving licence (permiso de la clase B) in Spain, you must meet these requirements:

- **Age / Edad:** You must be at least 18 years old (tener al menos dieciocho años). You can sit the theory exam up to 3 months before your 18th birthday, but you cannot take the practical exam until you turn 18.

- **Residency / Residencia:** You must be a legal resident in Spain (tener residencia legal en España). Your NIE or TIE serves as proof.

- **Medical fitness / Aptitud médica:** You must pass the psicotécnico at a DGT-authorised medical centre (centro de reconocimiento de conductores — CRC).

- **Theory exam / Examen teórico:** 30 multiple-choice questions in 30 minutes. Maximum 3 errors allowed (máximo tres fallos). Available in English — request this when booking.

- **Practical exam / Examen práctico:** Approximately 25 minutes on public roads in a dual-control car from a registered driving school. Conducted in Spanish by a DGT examiner.

---

### The Psicotécnico — Step by Step

The psicotécnico (also called the reconocimiento médico) is a mandatory medical and psychological assessment. It's quick and straightforward.

| Prueba                  | Test                     | What Happens                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Prueba visual           | Vision test              | Snellen chart (reading letters at distance), colour perception, glare recovery |
| Prueba auditiva         | Hearing test             | Detecting sounds at different frequencies in a soundproof room                 |
| Coordinación y reflejos | Coordination & reflexes  | Following a moving target on screen, pressing buttons in response to stimuli   |
| Cuestionario médico     | Medical questionnaire    | Questions about medical history, medication, conditions                        |
| Evaluación psicológica  | Psychological assessment | Brief interview about mental health, stress, attention                         |

**Key facts:**

- Cost: €30–80 depending on the centre
- Duration: 15–30 minutes
- Certificate validity: **3 months** (extends to 2 years once you've sat any DGT exam)
- What to bring: NIE/TIE or passport, glasses/contact lenses if you wear them
- The centre transmits your results directly to the DGT electronically

**Useful phrase at the CRC:**

> _Vengo para hacerme el psicotécnico para el permiso de conducir clase B._
> I've come to do the medical for the class B driving licence.

### The Autoescuela — How It Works

An autoescuela (driving school) is required for the practical exam because you must use a dual-control car (coche de doble mando). For the theory exam, self-study is permitted, but most people study through an autoescuela or an online platform.

**What the autoescuela provides:**

- Theory classes (clases teóricas) — in-person or online
- Practice tests (test de práctica) — DGT-format questions
- Practical driving lessons (clases prácticas) — with a qualified instructor
- Exam booking and administration — they handle the paperwork with the DGT

**There is no fixed minimum** number of practical lessons required. Your instructor will recommend when you're ready. The average is around 20 lessons, but this varies.

### The Theory Exam — Format & Facts

| Detail    | Spanish           | Info                                                  |
| --------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Questions | Preguntas         | 30 multiple-choice (tipo test)                        |
| Time      | Tiempo            | 30 minutes (treinta minutos)                          |
| Pass mark | Nota para aprobar | Maximum 3 errors (máximo tres fallos)                 |
| Language  | Idioma            | English available — request when booking              |
| Location  | Lugar             | At the DGT office or via your autoescuela             |
| Results   | Resultados        | Available from 17:00 the same day or next working day |

**What the questions cover:** Road signs (señales de tráfico), priority rules (prioridad), speed limits (límites de velocidad), alcohol & drug limits (límites de alcohol y drogas), vehicle safety (seguridad del vehículo), road markings (marcas viales), and safe driving behaviour (conducción segura).

**If you fail:** You can retake the exam. The waiting time depends on how many errors you made. Your autoescuela will rebook you.

### The Practical Exam — What to Expect

| Detail     | Spanish       | Info                                               |
| ---------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Duration   | Duración      | ~25 minutes (veinticinco minutos)                  |
| Vehicle    | Vehículo      | Dual-control car from your autoescuela             |
| Examiner   | Examinador(a) | DGT official, sits in the passenger seat           |
| Language   | Idioma        | Conducted in **Spanish**                           |
| Route      | Recorrido     | Mixed roads — urban, interurban, possibly motorway |
| Manoeuvres | Maniobras     | Parallel parking, hill starts, three-point turns   |

**Top reasons for failing the practical exam:**

1. Observation errors (errores de observación) — not checking mirrors
2. Not respecting priority (no respetar la prioridad)
3. Poor positioning on the road (mala colocación en la calzada)

**After passing:** Your autoescuela gives you a provisional licence (permiso provisional) valid for 3 months. The plastic card arrives by post within about 6 weeks. New drivers must display a green **"L" sign** (señal de la L verde) on the car for the first year.

## 🗣️ Conjugation Spotlight

### APROBAR — to pass (an exam)

This is a **stem-changing verb** (o → ue) in the present tense.

|             | Presente  | Pretérito   | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| yo          | apruebo   | aprobé      | aprobaba    |
| tú          | apruebas  | aprobaste   | aprobabas   |
| él/ella     | aprueba   | aprobó      | aprobaba    |
| nosotros    | aprobamos | aprobamos   | aprobábamos |

## 🏙️ Valencia Connection

In Valencia, the DGT office (Jefatura Provincial de Tráfico) is located in the Quatre Carreres district. Your autoescuela will handle most of the interaction with this office. The practical exam routes in Valencia typically cover a mix of city streets near the exam centre, possibly including sections along Avenida del Puerto, the ring roads, and residential areas. Each province uses 2–3 fixed circuits, so your instructor will practise these specific routes with you.

Valencia also has a **ZBE** (Zona de Bajas Emisiones — low emission zone) which is increasingly relevant to drivers and appears in newer DGT questions. We'll cover this in detail in Topic 11.

## ⚠️ UK → Spain Differences for This Topic

| UK                                                   | Spain                                                         | Watch Out                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| No medical exam needed for standard licence          | Psicotécnico is **mandatory**                                 | Must be done before any exam                               |
| Theory test at DVSA centres                          | Theory test at DGT office or autoescuela                      | You choose the language when booking                       |
| Can practise with any qualified driver               | Must practise with autoescuela instructor in dual-control car | No practising with friends/family                          |
| Provisional licence lets you drive with a supervisor | No equivalent — you can't drive until you pass both exams     | Don't drive on your US licence after 6 months of residency |
| Pass certificate valid 2 years                       | Theory pass valid — check with your autoescuela               | Practical must follow within reasonable timeframe          |
| Points start at 0, you accumulate them as penalties  | Points start at **8** — you **lose** them for offences        | Reversed system!                                           |
| Green L-plates for learners                          | Green L-plate for **new drivers** (first year after passing)  | Different meaning of the L-plate                           |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **No exchange agreement** between the US and Spain — you must take both exams.
2. **Psicotécnico first** — it's your gateway to everything else. Certificate valid 3 months.
3. **Theory exam:** 30 questions, max 3 wrong, available in English.
4. **Practical exam:** ~25 minutes, in Spanish, in your autoescuela's car.
5. **New drivers start with 8 points** and must display a green L for one year.
6. **Key verbs to master:** aprobar, suspender, matricularse, conducir.
