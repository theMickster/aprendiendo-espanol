# 26 — Practical Exam Commands / Instrucciones del Examen Práctico

## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (CRITICAL — The Practical Exam Is in Spanish!)

## 🎯 What This Topic Covers

The DGT practical exam is conducted **entirely in Spanish**. The examiner sits in the back seat and gives you instructions. Your driving instructor sits in the passenger seat with dual controls. There are only about **12–15 standard commands** the examiner uses — learn them by heart and you'll understand everything. This page is your **most valuable study tool** for the practical exam.

---

## 🚗 BEFORE YOU START — Antes de Arrancar

The examiner will say these phrases before you begin driving:

| Spanish Command                                            | English                                          | Pronunciation Guide                                                                       | What to Do                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| _Asegúrate de que tienes bien colocados los retrovisores._ | Make sure your mirrors are correctly positioned. | ah-seh-GOO-rah-teh deh keh tee-EH-nehs bee-EN koh-loh-KAH-dohs lohs reh-troh-bee-SOH-rehs | Adjust all 3 mirrors              |
| _Ponte el cinturón de seguridad._                          | Put on your seatbelt.                            | PON-teh el theen-too-ROHN                                                                 | Fasten seatbelt                   |
| _Arranca el coche._                                        | Start the car.                                   | ah-RRAN-kah el KOH-cheh                                                                   | Turn the key / press start        |
| _¿Estás listo/lista?_                                      | Are you ready?                                   | es-TAHS LEES-toh/LEES-tah                                                                 | Say "Sí" and wait for instruction |

**💡 Pro tip:** Do the mirrors and seatbelt AUTOMATICALLY when you sit down — before the examiner even asks. This shows confidence and good habits.

## ➡️ DIRECTION COMMANDS — Instrucciones de Dirección

These are the commands you'll hear MOST during the exam:

| Spanish Command                          | English                                | Pronunciation                   | What to Do                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| _Siga todo recto._                       | Go straight ahead.                     | SEE-gah TOH-doh RREK-toh        | Continue straight                 |
| _Siga recto._                            | Go straight.                           | SEE-gah RREK-toh                | Same as above (shorter)           |
| _Gire a la derecha._                     | Turn right.                            | HEE-reh ah lah deh-REH-chah     | Signal right, turn right          |
| _Gire a la izquierda._                   | Turn left.                             | HEE-reh ah lah eeth-kee-EHR-dah | Signal left, turn left            |
| _En la próxima, gire a la derecha._      | At the next one, turn right.           | en lah PROK-see-mah             | Next intersection, turn right     |
| _En la próxima, gire a la izquierda._    | At the next one, turn left.            |                                 | Next intersection, turn left      |
| _Tome la primera a la derecha._          | Take the first right.                  | TOH-meh lah pree-MEH-rah        | First available right turn        |
| _Tome la segunda a la izquierda._        | Take the second left.                  | seh-GOON-dah                    | Second available left turn        |
| _Tome la salida._                        | Take the exit.                         | sah-LEE-dah                     | Exit the roundabout/motorway      |
| _Cambie de sentido cuando pueda._        | Make a U-turn when you can.            | KAM-bee-eh deh sen-TEE-doh      | Legal U-turn when safe            |
| _Siga las indicaciones hacia [destino]._ | Follow the signs toward [destination]. | een-dee-kah-THYOH-nehs          | Read road signs, follow direction |

### 🚨 KEY PHRASE: "Siga todo recto"

This is the default instruction. If the examiner says nothing, **continue straight ahead** following the road. You don't need a new instruction at every intersection if you're going straight on a priority road.

## ❌ TOP REASONS FOR FAILING — Fallos Eliminatorios

These are **instant-fail** errors — just ONE of these and you fail the entire exam:

| Error                                                   | Spanish                       | Why It's Fatal                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 🚫 Running a red light                                  | Saltarse un semáforo en rojo  | Traffic violation                  |
| 🚫 Ignoring a STOP sign                                 | No respetar una señal de STOP | Traffic violation                  |
| 🚫 Entering a no-entry street                           | Entrar en dirección prohibida | The R-101 trap — very common!      |
| 🚫 Causing the examiner to intervene with dual controls | Intervención del examinador   | They had to brake or steer for you |
| 🚫 Mounting the pavement                                | Subirse a la acera            | Danger to pedestrians              |
| 🚫 Not yielding to pedestrians at a crossing            | No ceder el paso a peatones   | Priority violation                 |
| 🚫 Dangerous overtaking                                 | Adelantamiento peligroso      | Safety risk                        |
| 🚫 Excessive speed                                      | Exceso de velocidad           | Breaking the law                   |
| 🚫 Driving into oncoming traffic                        | Invadir el sentido contrario  | Extreme danger                     |

### Common Non-Fatal Errors (but too many = fail)

- Not checking mirrors enough
- Not checking blind spot before lane changes
- Stalling the engine (1–2 times is OK, repeated stalling is a problem)
- Positioning poorly in the lane
- Being hesitant or overly cautious (blocking traffic)
- Forgetting to signal
- Choosing the wrong gear

**You can make up to 9 minor (_leve_) errors. 1 serious (_deficiente_) error + 4 minor = fail. 1 eliminatory error = instant fail.**

## 🗣️ PHRASES FOR YOU TO SAY

You won't need to say much, but these phrases help:

| Situation                       | Spanish                     | English                            |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| You didn't hear the instruction | _Perdone, ¿puede repetir?_  | Sorry, can you repeat?             |
| You're not sure which turn      | _¿La primera o la segunda?_ | The first or the second?           |
| You're confirming               | _¿A la derecha?_            | To the right?                      |
| You've finished parking         | _Ya está._                  | Done. / That's it.                 |
| You're ready                    | _Estoy listo._              | I'm ready. (Use _lista_ if female) |
| Thank you after the test        | _Muchas gracias._           | Thank you very much.               |

**💡 Mick's critical phrase:** _"Perdone, ¿puede repetir?"_ — This is your lifeline. If you don't understand an instruction, ASK. The examiner will repeat it. It's better to ask than to guess wrong.

## 📋 THE EXAM DAY — What Actually Happens

| Step | What Happens                                                                                                  |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Arrive at the autoescuela at your scheduled time                                                              |
| 2    | Wait with 4–5 other candidates — the examiner takes you as a group                                            |
| 3    | When it's your turn, get in the driver's seat. Instructor = passenger. Examiner = back seat                   |
| 4    | Other candidates from your group sit in the back as observers while you drive — ignore them, focus on driving |
| 5    | Adjust mirrors, seatbelt, seat position                                                                       |
| 6    | The examiner gives instructions. Drive for ~25 minutes                                                        |
| 7    | Routes are in the local area — your instructor should have practised these exact streets with you             |
| 8    | You'll do urban driving, possibly some interurban, maybe motorway entry/exit                                  |
| 9    | At least one parking manoeuvre (parallel, bay, or hill start)                                                 |
| 10   | Examiner says "Hemos terminado" or "Volvemos a la autoescuela"                                                |
| 11   | Results available from 17:00 same day or next working day via sede.dgt.gob.es                                 |

**Exam duration:** ~25 minutes of driving

**Pass/fail threshold:** Max 9 minor errors, 0 eliminatory errors, 0 deficient errors (or 1 deficient + max 4 minor)

## 🏙️ Valencia Connection

- **Exam routes in Valencia** cover the streets near the DGT office in Quatre Carreres — your instructor will practise these specific routes with you
- **Common route features:** Roundabouts on Av. de las Balears, pedestrian crossings near schools, parallel parking on side streets, entering/exiting the V-30
- **R-101 (no entry) traps:** MANY one-way streets in the old town area near the exam routes — watch for the red bar sign!
- **Pedestrian crossings:** Near Ciudad de las Artes — the examiner watches closely for yield behaviour here
- **Lane discipline:** On Av. del Puerto and multi-lane roads — stay in the correct lane for your intended direction

## ⚠️ US → Spain Practical Exam Differences

| US                           | Spain                                                         | Key Difference              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Exam in English              | Exam in **SPANISH only**                                      | Must learn the ~15 commands |
| Usually 1-on-1 with examiner | **Group exam** — other candidates in the back seat            | Can be distracting          |
| Your own car or DMV car      | **Autoescuela's car** (dual control) — you MUST use their car | No choice of vehicle        |
| Examiner in passenger seat   | **Examiner in BACK seat** — instructor in passenger seat      | Different dynamic           |
| ~15–20 min in many states    | **~25 minutes**                                               | Slightly longer             |
| Instructor doesn't attend    | **Instructor attends** — sits beside you                      | They know your weaknesses   |
| Usually 1 attempt per visit  | Results same day or next — rebook through autoescuela         | Different process           |
| Parallel park common         | Parallel park + bay parking + possible hill start             | More parking variations     |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **The exam is in Spanish.** Learn the ~15 standard commands by heart.
2. **"Siga todo recto"** = keep going straight. Default if no instruction given.
3. **"Gire a la derecha/izquierda"** = turn right/left. Always signal first!
4. **"Perdone, ¿puede repetir?"** = your lifeline if you don't understand.
5. **Instant-fail errors:** red light, STOP sign, no-entry, examiner intervention, mounting pavement, not yielding to pedestrians.
6. **Max 9 minor errors.** 1 eliminatory = instant fail.
7. **Do mirrors + seatbelt automatically** before the examiner asks.
8. **R-101 (no entry) is the #1 trap** — watch for the red bar sign at EVERY intersection.
9. **Practice the exact exam routes** with your instructor — they know them.
10. **Stay calm.** The examiner wants to see **safe, normal driving** — not perfection. Drive like you would with your family in the car.
