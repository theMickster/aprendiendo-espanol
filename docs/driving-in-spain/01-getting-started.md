## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐ (Foundation)

## 🎯 What This Topic Covers

This topic walks you through the **entire process** of getting your Spanish driving licence (permiso de conducir) from scratch — the steps, the paperwork, the medical, and how the exam system works. You'll also learn the essential vocabulary you need just to navigate the system itself: talking to the autoescuela, booking the psicotécnico, and understanding what the DGT expects.

---

## 🗺️ The Process at a Glance

| Step | Spanish                         | English                     | Notes                       |
| ---- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1    | Matricularse en una autoescuela | Enrol at a driving school   | Required for practical test |
| 2    | Pasar el psicotécnico           | Pass the medical exam       | €30–80, lasts 20–30 min     |
| 3    | Estudiar la teoría              | Study the theory            | Online or in-class          |
| 4    | Aprobar el examen teórico       | Pass the theory exam        | 30 questions, max 3 errors  |
| 5    | Tomar clases prácticas          | Take practical lessons      | No fixed minimum hours      |
| 6    | Aprobar el examen práctico      | Pass the practical exam     | ~25 min on real roads       |
| 7    | Recibir el permiso provisional  | Receive provisional licence | Valid 3 months              |
| 8    | Recibir el carnet definitivo    | Receive final licence       | Arrives by post, ~6 weeks   |

## 💡 Why This Matters for You, Mick

As a US licence holder living in Valencia, there's **no exchange agreement** between the US and Spain. That means you need to pass both the theory and practical exams from scratch. The good news: the theory exam is available in English in Valencia. The practical exam is in Spanish, but the examiner uses standard, predictable commands — and we'll drill those in Topic 26.

Your psicotécnico certificate is valid for **3 months** from issue. Once you've sat for any exam (even if you fail), the validity extends to **2 years**. So: do the psicotécnico first, then get studying.

**🆕 2026 heads-up:** DGT is phasing in **risk-perception videos** in the theory exam (still 30 Q / max 3 errors). The new **sign catalogue (RD 465/2025)** has been on exams since 1 October 2025. The **baliza V-16 conectada** is mandatory from 1 January 2026 — warning triangles are no longer valid. These all appear throughout the topics below.
