## 🎯 What This Section Covers

This section covers three remaining signal categories: **informational signs** (blue or white rectangles that guide and inform), **traffic lights** (semáforos), and **police/officer signals** (the highest authority on the road). Together with sections 06a and 06b, this completes the full DGT traffic signs curriculum.

> **🆕 2025 Sign Catalogue Update (RD 465/2025)** — In force since 1 July 2025, on DGT theory exams since 1 October 2025. Informational-sign changes include new tunnel emergency pictograms, environmental-restriction signs for ZBE / low-emission zones, new coexistence-zone variants (S-28 family), and updated VMP path / no-entry signs. ## ℹ️ INFORMATIONAL SIGNS — Señales de Indicación

Informational signs **do not prohibit or oblige** — they provide useful information. They're typically blue or white rectangles (or squares).

### Road Type Indicators

| Code      | Shape & Colour                        | Spanish Name           | English                      | What It Tells You                               |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **S-1**   | 🟦 Blue rectangle, white "A" + number | Autopista              | Motorway                     | You're entering/on a motorway (may be tolled)   |
| **S-2**   | 🟦 Blue rectangle, white text         | Autovía                | Dual carriageway             | Free motorway-standard road                     |
| **S-3**   | 🟩 Green rectangle                    | Vía para automóviles   | Road for motor vehicles only | No pedestrians, cyclists, or mopeds allowed     |
| **S-220** | ⬜ White rectangle                    | Zona residencial       | Residential zone             | Max 20 km/h, pedestrians have absolute priority |
| **S-28**  | 🟦 Blue rectangle, "30"               | Zona 30                | 30 km/h zone                 | Speed limit 30, shared space                    |
| **S-30**  | 🟦 Blue arrow                         | Calle de sentido único | One-way street               | Traffic flows in the arrow's direction          |
| **S-13**  | 🟦 Blue rectangle "P"                 | Estacionamiento        | Parking                      | Parking area indicated                          |
| **S-17**  | 🟦 Blue "H"                           | Hospital               | Hospital nearby              | Drive quietly, be alert for ambulances          |

### Direction & Destination Signs

| Colour               | Type                                            | Usage                                           |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Blue background**  | Motorway/autovía destinations                   | E.g., "A-7 → Barcelona 350 km"                  |
| **Green background** | Main road (carretera convencional) destinations | E.g., "N-332 → Alicante 180 km"                 |
| **White background** | Local destinations, urban directions            | E.g., "Centro Ciudad →"                         |
| **Brown background** | Tourist/cultural points of interest             | E.g., "Ciudad de las Artes y las Ciencias →" 🏛️ |
| **Yellow/Orange**    | Temporary diversions or roadworks routes        | E.g., "Desvío →"                                |

### 2026 Updates — New Informational Signs

| Sign               | Spanish                        | English                         | Why It's New                                                |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| ⚡ **EV Charging** | Punto de recarga eléctrica     | Electric vehicle charging point | Growing EV infrastructure across Spain                      |
| 🛴 **VMP Zone**    | Zona VMP                       | Personal mobility vehicle zone  | Regulating patinetes (electric scooters)                    |
| 🟢 **ZBE Entry**   | Zona de Bajas Emisiones        | Low Emission Zone entry         | Now standardised nationally — you'll see these in Valencia! |
| 🔴 **ZBE End**     | Fin de Zona de Bajas Emisiones | End of Low Emission Zone        | Marks where restrictions end                                |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Traps — Informational Signs

1. **S-1 (autopista) vs S-2 (autovía):** Both look similar (blue background). The key: autopistas may charge tolls (peaje). Autovías are ALWAYS free.
2. **S-220 (residential zone):** Speed limit is 20 km/h AND pedestrians have ABSOLUTE priority everywhere — not just at crossings.
3. **Direction sign colours:** Blue = motorway. Green = main road. White = local. Brown = tourist. The DGT tests this!
4. **One-way sign (S-30) vs No entry (R-101):** S-30 tells you the DIRECTION of flow (placed at the start). R-101 tells you that you CANNOT enter (placed at the blocked end).

## 🚦 TRAFFIC LIGHTS — Semáforos

Traffic lights rank **3rd** in the signal hierarchy — above vertical signs and road markings, but below police officers and temporary signs.

### Standard Vehicle Traffic Lights

| Light                   | Spanish            | What to Do                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 **Red (solid)**      | Rojo fijo          | **STOP.** Do not pass the stop line. Do not enter the intersection.                                                                                                  |
| 🟡 **Amber (solid)**    | Ámbar fijo         | **STOP** if you can safely. If already in the intersection or too close to stop safely, proceed with caution.                                                        |
| 🟢 **Green (solid)**    | Verde fijo         | **GO** — but yield to pedestrians on crossings and vehicles still in the intersection.                                                                               |
| 🟢➡️ **Green arrow**    | Flecha verde       | GO in the arrow's direction ONLY. Other directions are prohibited.                                                                                                   |
| 🔴➡️ **Red arrow**      | Flecha roja        | STOP for the arrow's direction. Other directions may be allowed by other signals.                                                                                    |
| 🟡⚡ **Flashing amber** | Ámbar intermitente | **CAUTION** — proceed carefully, yielding to traffic that has priority. The traffic light is not actively controlling — drive as if at an uncontrolled intersection. |
| 🔴⚡ **Flashing red**   | Rojo intermitente  | **STOP** — equivalent to a STOP sign. Stop completely, then proceed when safe.                                                                                       |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Traps — Traffic Lights

1. **Amber does NOT mean "speed up."** If you can stop safely, you MUST stop. Only continue through if stopping would be dangerous.
2. **Flashing amber ≠ flashing red.** Flashing amber = caution/yield. Flashing red = STOP completely.
3. **Green light does NOT mean "go blindly."** You must still yield to pedestrians on crossings and vehicles clearing the intersection.
4. **Right turn on green:** Even when the light is green, you CANNOT turn right if a RED ARROW is showing for your direction.
5. **Right turn on red: NEVER ALLOWED.** Unlike the US, a red light means NO movement in ANY direction unless a green arrow says otherwise.

### Pedestrian Traffic Lights

| Light                            | Spanish                   | What It Means                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🔴🚶 **Red figure**              | Peatón rojo               | Pedestrians STOP — do not cross                                    |
| 🟢🚶 **Green figure**            | Peatón verde              | Pedestrians may cross                                              |
| 🟢🚶⚡ **Flashing green figure** | Peatón verde intermitente | Pedestrians already crossing should FINISH. Do NOT start crossing. |

### Lane-Specific Traffic Lights (Reversible Lanes)

| Light                              | Spanish                  | What It Means                                |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| 🟢⬇️ **Green arrow pointing down** | Flecha verde hacia abajo | This lane is OPEN for your direction         |
| 🔴❌ **Red X**                     | Aspa roja                | This lane is CLOSED — do not enter           |
| 🟡↙️ **Amber diagonal arrow**      | Flecha ámbar diagonal    | Lane is closing — move to the indicated lane |

### Special Traffic Lights

| Type                           | Spanish                  | Where You See It                                      |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 🚂 **Railway crossing lights** | Semáforo de paso a nivel | Two alternating red lights = STOP. Train approaching! |
| 🚌 **Bus-only light**          | Semáforo de carril bus   | White bar/arrow on black = bus may proceed            |
| 🚲 **Cycle light**             | Semáforo para ciclistas  | Bicycle symbol — controls cycle lane                  |
| 🚶 **Pedestrian light**        | Semáforo para peatones   | Person symbol — controls crossing                     |

## 👮 POLICE/OFFICER SIGNALS — Señales de los Agentes

Police officer signals are the **#1 highest authority** on the road. They override EVERYTHING — traffic lights, signs, road markings, all of it.

### The Officer's Position & Arms

| Officer Position                                             | What It Means for YOU                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 👮 **Facing you or back to you** (arms out to sides or down) | **STOP.** You cannot proceed.                                                                                       |
| 👮 **Side-on to you** (arms out to sides or down)            | **GO.** You may proceed in front of them.                                                                           |
| 👮 **Arm raised vertically**                                 | **STOP for ALL directions.** Equivalent to amber — stop if you can, clear the intersection if you're already in it. |
| 👮 **Arm extended horizontally pointing at you**             | **STOP.** That arm is like a barrier.                                                                               |
| 👮 **Waving/beckoning you**                                  | **Proceed** as directed.                                                                                            |
| 👮 **Swinging a red light or flag**                          | **STOP immediately.**                                                                                               |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Traps — Police Signals

1. **Officer facing you = STOP.** Think of their CHEST as a wall. If you see their chest (or back), you cannot go.
2. **Officer side-on = GO.** If you see their SHOULDER, you may proceed in front of them.
3. **Raised arm = ALL STOP.** Everyone in every direction stops (like a universal amber phase).
4. **Officer overrides red light:** If the light is red but the officer waves you through, you GO. The officer always wins.
5. **Officer overrides green light:** If the light is green but the officer holds up their hand to stop you, you STOP.

**💡 Mick's Memory Trick:**

- **Chest/Back = Barrier = STOP** 🛑
- **Shoulder = Sliding door = GO** ✅
- **Arm up = Everybody freeze** ✋

## 🔔 The V-16 Beacon — NEW for 2026

As of **January 1, 2026**, the traditional emergency triangles are no longer the legal standard. The new **baliza V-16** is now mandatory:

| Feature            | Details                                                   |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **What it is**     | An amber flashing beacon you place on your roof           |
| **Replaces**       | Emergency triangles on motorways/autovías                 |
| **Why**            | To avoid drivers being hit while placing triangles        |
| **Colour**         | Amber/yellow, 360° visibility                             |
| **Connectivity**   | Must connect to DGT 3.0 platform, sends your GPS location |
| **Where to place** | On the highest point of your vehicle roof                 |
| **Key advantage**  | You never leave the vehicle — safer!                      |

**DGT exam relevance:** Expect questions about the V-16 on the 2026 exam. Know that it replaces triangles, it's amber, goes on the roof, and transmits location.

## 🗣️ Conjugation Spotlight

### OBEDECER — to obey

An irregular verb: **c → zc** in the yo form of the present tense.

|             | Presente   | Pretérito    | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | ---------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| yo          | obedezco   | obedecí      | obedecía    |
| tú          | obedeces   | obedeciste   | obedecías   |
| él/ella     | obedece    | obedeció     | obedecía    |
| nosotros    | obedecemos | obedecimos   | obedecíamos |
| vosotros    | obedecéis  | obedecisteis | obedecíais  |
| ellos/ellas | obedecen   | obedecieron  | obedecían   |

**Key sentences:**

- _Siempre debes obedecer al agente de tráfico._ — You must always obey the traffic officer.
- _No obedecí la señal y me pusieron una multa._ — I didn't obey the sign and I got a fine.
- _Obedezco las señales de tráfico._ — I obey the traffic signs.

### INDICAR — to indicate / to show

|             | Presente  | Pretérito   | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| yo          | indico    | indiqué     | indicaba    |
| tú          | indicas   | indicaste   | indicabas   |
| él/ella     | indica    | indicó      | indicaba    |
| nosotros    | indicamos | indicamos   | indicábamos |
| vosotros    | indicáis  | indicasteis | indicabais  |
| ellos/ellas | indican   | indicaron   | indicaban   |

**Note:** _Indicar_ has a **c→qu** change in preterite yo: _indiqué_ (not ~~indicé~~).

**Key sentences:**

- _La señal indica la dirección a la autopista._ — The sign indicates the direction to the motorway.
- _El agente indicó que parara._ — The officer indicated I should stop.

## ⚠️ US → Spain Differences

| US                                    | Spain                                                      | Key Difference                                                     |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Green = go, no conditions             | Green = go, BUT yield to pedestrians and clearing vehicles | Spain requires active awareness on green                           |
| Right on red usually OK               | Right on red **NEVER** OK                                  | #1 mistake Americans make in Spain                                 |
| Flashing yellow = caution             | Flashing amber = caution/yield                             | Same concept, different name                                       |
| Flashing red = stop sign equivalent   | Flashing red = stop sign equivalent                        | Same!                                                              |
| Police rarely direct traffic manually | Police direct traffic at events, accidents, Fallas         | Be ready for hand signals                                          |
| Emergency triangles behind car        | **V-16 beacon on roof (2026)**                             | Triangles are OUT, beacon is IN                                    |
| Interstate signs = green              | Motorway signs = blue (autopista/autovía)                  | Reversed colours! Green = conventional road in Spain               |
| Brown signs = recreation              | Brown signs = tourist/cultural interest                    | Similar concept                                                    |
| White signs = speed limits            | White signs = local directions                             | Different use of white                                             |
| Yellow signs = warning                | Yellow/orange signs = TEMPORARY/roadworks                  | Yellow permanent warnings DON'T exist in Spain — they're triangles |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **Informational signs are rectangular** — blue, green, white, or brown backgrounds.
2. **Blue = motorway. Green = main road. White = local. Brown = tourist.** The DGT tests sign colours!
3. **S-220 (residential zone) = 20 km/h** and pedestrians have ABSOLUTE priority.
4. **Amber light = STOP if safe.** It does NOT mean "speed up."
5. **Flashing amber = caution/yield. Flashing red = STOP completely.**
6. **Green light ≠ "go blindly."** Still yield to pedestrians and clearing vehicles.
7. **Officer facing you (chest/back) = STOP.** Officer side-on (shoulder) = GO.
8. **Police officer overrides EVERYTHING** — even traffic lights and signs.
9. **V-16 beacon replaces emergency triangles as of January 2026.** Goes on roof, amber light, sends GPS.
10. **Right turn on red: NEVER allowed in Spain.** Never, never, never.
