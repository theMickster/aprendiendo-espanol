# 06a — Regulatory Traffic Signs / Señales de Reglamentación

## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ (Core Exam Knowledge)

## 🎯 What This Section Covers

Regulatory signs tell you what you **MUST** or **MUST NOT** do. They are the rules of the road in visual form. The DGT divides them into three families: **priority signs**, **prohibition signs**, and **obligation signs**. Together, they make up roughly 30–40% of all DGT exam questions — making this the single most important section in the entire wiki.

---

## 🔑 The Shape-Colour Code — Your First Shortcut

Before memorizing individual signs, learn this universal European system. Once you know the shape and colour, you can guess what ANY sign means — even one you've never seen before.

| Shape                | Border / Background           | Meaning                                              | Think of It As…                     |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 🔺 Inverted triangle | White with red border         | **YIELD / Give way**                                 | "Careful — someone else goes first" |
| 🛑 Octagon           | Red with white text           | **STOP**                                             | Same as the US!                     |
| 🔴 Circle            | White with red border         | **PROHIBITION** — you CANNOT do this                 | "Red ring = NO"                     |
| 🔵 Circle            | Blue background, white symbol | **OBLIGATION** — you MUST do this                    | "Blue = DO"                         |
| 🔶 Diamond (yellow)  | Yellow with white border      | **PRIORITY ROAD**                                    | "You have right of way"             |
| ⬜ Square/rectangle  | Blue or white                 | **END of restriction** — usually with diagonal lines | "Restriction over"                  |

**💡 Mick's Memory Trick:**

- **Red ring** = **STOP doing it** (prohibition) 🚫
- **Blue circle** = **START doing it** (obligation) ✅
- This is the OPPOSITE of US road sign logic where blue = informational!

## 🛑 FAMILY 1: Priority Signs — Señales de Prioridad

Priority signs have **unique shapes** so they can be recognised from behind, at night, or when damaged. This is why STOP is an octagon and YIELD is an inverted triangle — no other signs share these shapes.

### The Essential Priority Signs

| Code    | Shape & Colour                  | Spanish Name                                                  | English                        | What It Means                                                                                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **R-1** | 🔺 Inverted triangle, white/red | Ceda el paso                                                  | Give way / Yield               | Slow down, yield to traffic on the road you're joining. You do NOT have to stop if the way is clear. |
| **R-2** | 🛑 Octagon, red/white           | Detención obligatoria (STOP)                                  | Stop                           | You MUST stop completely at the line (or before the intersection if no line), then yield.            |
| **P-1** | 🔶 Yellow diamond               | Calzada con prioridad                                         | Priority road                  | You have priority over joining traffic. Stays in force until cancelled by P-2.                       |
| **P-2** | 🔶 with diagonal lines          | Fin de prioridad                                              | End of priority                | You no longer have priority — default rules apply again.                                             |
| **P-3** | 🔶 with arrow                   | Prioridad en sentido contrario                                | Priority for oncoming traffic  | The OTHER direction has priority. YOU must wait.                                                     |
| **P-4** | 🔶 Red arrow + black arrow      | Prioridad respecto al sentido contrario                       | Priority over oncoming traffic | YOU have priority. The other direction must wait.                                                    |
| **P-6** | 🔵 Circle with arrows           | Intersección con prioridad sobre incorporación por la derecha | Priority over right            | You have priority at the NEXT intersection, overriding the default "right has priority" rule.        |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Traps — Priority Signs

1. **R-1 vs R-2:** At a YIELD (R-1) you only stop IF there's traffic. At a STOP (R-2) you MUST stop even if the road is completely empty.
2. **P-3 vs P-4:** These look similar! P-3 (red arrow = you) means you WAIT. P-4 (red arrow = them) means YOU go. Remember: **red arrow = whoever must wait**.
3. **P-6 only works at the NEXT intersection.** After that, default rules return.

### 🇺🇸→🇪🇸 US vs Spain Difference

| US                                  | Spain                               | Watch Out                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 4-way stop common                   | 4-way stop almost NEVER exists      | In Spain, one road ALWAYS has priority |
| Yellow diamond = warning            | Yellow diamond = **PRIORITY ROAD**  | Completely different meaning!          |
| Right turn on red allowed (usually) | Right turn on red **NEVER** allowed | Even when the road is clear — NEVER    |

## 🔴 FAMILY 2: Prohibition Signs — Señales de Prohibición

All prohibition signs are **round, white background, red border**. The symbol inside shows what is forbidden. A red diagonal bar through the symbol reinforces the prohibition.

### Entry & Access Prohibitions

| Code      | Symbol                           | Spanish Name                           | English                        | DGT Notes                                                                        |
| --------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **R-100** | White circle, red border (empty) | Circulación prohibida                  | No traffic in either direction | Applies to ALL vehicles AND pedestrians                                          |
| **R-101** | White with red bar               | Entrada prohibida                      | No entry                       | One-way street — you can't enter from this side, but traffic flows the other way |
| **R-102** | 🚗 silhouette                    | Entrada prohibida a vehículos de motor | No motor vehicles              | Bicycles and pedestrians CAN still enter                                         |
| **R-104** | 🏍️ silhouette                    | Entrada prohibida a motocicletas       | No motorcycles                 | Applies to all motorcycles                                                       |
| **R-114** | 🚲 silhouette                    | Entrada prohibida a ciclos             | No bicycles                    | New 2026 design: modernised bicycle pictogram                                    |
| **R-116** | 🛴 silhouette                    | Entrada prohibida a VMP                | No personal mobility vehicles  | **NEW 2026** — specific to electric scooters/patinetes                           |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — R-100 vs R-101

This is the #1 most confused pair on the DGT exam:

- **R-100** (empty circle) = **NO traffic EITHER direction** — road is closed entirely
- **R-101** (red bar) = **No ENTRY from this side** — but traffic flows the other way (one-way street)

**Mick's Memory Trick:** The red BAR in R-101 is like a barrier at a one-way entrance — you can't go IN, but others come OUT.

### Manoeuvre Prohibitions

| Code      | Symbol | Spanish Name                                | English                       | DGT Notes                                                                             |
| --------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **R-200** | ⬅️❌   | Prohibición de adelantamiento               | No overtaking                 | Cannot pass the vehicle ahead. Ends at next "fin de prohibición" sign or intersection |
| **R-202** | ⬅️❌🚛 | Prohibición de adelantamiento para camiones | No overtaking for trucks      | Only applies to vehicles >3,500 kg                                                    |
| **R-302** | ➡️❌   | Giro a la derecha prohibido                 | No right turn                 |                                                                                       |
| **R-303** | ⬅️❌   | Giro a la izquierda prohibido               | No left turn                  | Also prohibits U-turns!                                                               |
| **R-304** | 🔄❌   | Media vuelta prohibida                      | No U-turn                     |                                                                                       |
| **R-305** | 🔊❌   | Advertencias acústicas prohibidas           | No horn / No audible warnings | Reminder of general rule — horn only to avoid accidents                               |

### Speed & Parking Prohibitions

| Code      | Symbol               | Spanish Name                       | English                    | DGT Notes                                                                                 |
| --------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **R-301** | Number in red circle | Velocidad máxima                   | Maximum speed              | Valid until cancelled by "end" sign, new speed sign, or intersection on non-priority road |
| **R-307** | 🅿️❌ with single bar | Parada y estacionamiento prohibido | No stopping AND no parking | Cannot stop AT ALL — not even briefly                                                     |
| **R-308** | 🅿️❌ with X          | Estacionamiento prohibido          | No parking                 | You CAN stop briefly (parada < 2 min) but NOT park                                        |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — R-307 vs R-308

- **R-307** (single diagonal red bar over blue background) = **NO stopping, NO parking** — you can't even pause
- **R-308** (X-shaped red bars over blue background) = **No PARKING** — but brief stops (parada < 2 min, driver stays) ARE allowed

### End of Prohibition Signs

| Code      | Symbol                                     | Spanish Name                         | English                   |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| **R-500** | ⚪ with diagonal black lines               | Fin de prohibiciones                 | End of ALL prohibitions   |
| **R-501** | Speed number with black lines              | Fin de limitación de velocidad       | End of speed limit        |
| **R-502** | ⚪ with diagonal lines + overtaking symbol | Fin de prohibición de adelantamiento | End of no-overtaking zone |

## 🔵 FAMILY 3: Obligation Signs — Señales de Obligación

All obligation signs are **round, blue background, white symbol**. They tell you what you MUST do.

### Direction Obligations

| Code      | Symbol   | Spanish Name                                  | English                         | DGT Notes                                         |
| --------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **R-400** | ⬆️       | Sentido obligatorio (recto)                   | Compulsory direction (straight) | You MUST go straight — no turning                 |
| **R-401** | ⬅️ or ➡️ | Paso obligatorio                              | Compulsory passing side         | Pass the obstacle/island on the indicated side    |
| **R-402** | ↩️       | Sentido obligatorio (derecha)                 | Compulsory right turn           | You MUST turn right                               |
| **R-403** | 🔄       | Intersección de sentido giratorio obligatorio | Compulsory roundabout direction | You MUST follow the roundabout — go anticlockwise |

### User & Vehicle Obligations

| Code      | Symbol         | Spanish Name                            | English                      | DGT Notes                                                         |
| --------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **R-407** | 🚲 on blue     | Camino reservado para ciclos            | Cycle path (mandatory)       | Cyclists MUST use this path. Other vehicles PROHIBITED            |
| **R-408** | 🚶+🚲          | Camino reservado para peatones y ciclos | Shared pedestrian/cycle path | Both share — be careful at crossings                              |
| **R-410** | 🚶 on blue     | Camino reservado para peatones          | Pedestrian path (mandatory)  | Pedestrians only                                                  |
| **R-411** | Number on blue | Velocidad mínima                        | Minimum speed                | You MUST drive AT LEAST this speed — slower vehicles cannot enter |
| **R-412** | ⛓️ on blue     | Cadenas para nieve obligatorias         | Snow chains compulsory       | Cannot continue without chains on at least one axle               |
| **R-413** | 💡 on blue     | Alumbrado de cruce obligatorio          | Dipped headlights compulsory | Must use dipped beams regardless of conditions                    |
| **R-416** | 🛴 on blue     | Camino reservado para VMP               | VMP path (mandatory)         | **NEW 2026** — for electric scooters/patinetes                    |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — Obligation Signs

- **R-411 (minimum speed):** If your vehicle CANNOT reach this speed, you are PROHIBITED from using this road. It's not a suggestion — it's a legal requirement.
- **R-407 vs advisory cycle lanes:** R-407 (blue circle) means cyclists MUST use it. A painted cycle lane on the road without this sign is advisory — cyclists may choose to use it or not.

## 📐 The Sign Hierarchy — Jerarquía de las Señales

When multiple signals conflict, you follow them in this strict order:

| Priority     | Signal Type                                | Spanish                                    | Example                                          |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **1st** 👮   | Police officer signals                     | Señales de los agentes                     | Officer waving you through overrides a red light |
| **2nd** 🚧   | Temporary signs & barriers                 | Señalización circunstancial y balizamiento | Orange/yellow temporary signs at roadworks       |
| **3rd** 🚦   | Traffic lights                             | Semáforos                                  | Red/amber/green lights                           |
| **4th** 🔴🔵 | Vertical signs (the ones in this section!) | Señales verticales                         | Stop, yield, speed limits, etc.                  |
| **5th** ═══  | Road markings                              | Marcas viales                              | Painted lines, arrows, crossings                 |

**💡 Memory Trick:** **P**olice → **T**emporary → **T**raffic lights → **V**ertical → **M**arkings
Think: **"Please Trust The Visible Marks"**

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — The Hierarchy

The DGT LOVES asking: "A police officer signals you to go, but the traffic light is red. What do you do?"

**Answer: ALWAYS follow the police officer.** They outrank everything else. Even if a sign says STOP and the officer waves you through — you GO.

## 🗣️ Conjugation Spotlight

### PROHIBIR — to prohibit / to forbid

An irregular verb: the **i** in the stem gets an accent in some present tense forms.

|             | Presente   | Pretérito    | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | ---------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| yo          | prohíbo    | prohibí      | prohibía    |
| tú          | prohíbes   | prohibiste   | prohibías   |
| él/ella     | prohíbe    | prohibió     | prohibía    |
| nosotros    | prohibimos | prohibimos   | prohibíamos |
| vosotros    | prohibís   | prohibisteis | prohibíais  |
| ellos/ellas | prohíben   | prohibieron  | prohibían   |

**Key sentences:**

- _Esta señal prohíbe el adelantamiento._ — This sign prohibits overtaking.
- _Está prohibido girar a la izquierda._ — Turning left is prohibited.
- _Se prohíbe la entrada a vehículos de motor._ — Entry for motor vehicles is prohibited.

### OBLIGAR — to oblige / to require

|             | Presente  | Pretérito   | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| yo          | obligo    | obligué     | obligaba    |
| tú          | obligas   | obligaste   | obligabas   |
| él/ella     | obliga    | obligó      | obligaba    |
| nosotros    | obligamos | obligamos   | obligábamos |
| vosotros    | obligáis  | obligasteis | obligabais  |
| ellos/ellas | obligan   | obligaron   | obligaban   |

**Note:** _Obligar_ has a **g→gu** spelling change in the preterite yo form: _obligué_ (not ~~obligé~~).

**Key sentences:**

- _La señal obliga a girar a la derecha._ — The sign requires turning right.
- _Es obligatorio usar el cinturón de seguridad._ — It is compulsory to use the seatbelt.

## 🏙️ Valencia Connection

You'll encounter these regulatory signs daily in Valencia:

- **R-101 (No entry):** All over the old town (Ciutat Vella) — many streets are one-way
- **R-301 (Speed limit 30):** Throughout residential zones including Penya-Roja and near the Turia
- **R-308 (No parking):** Along Avenida de Francia and near the Ciudad de las Artes
- **R-407 (Cycle path):** The extensive carril bici network through the Turia park
- **R-416 (VMP path):** New 2026 signs appearing for the many patinetes (scooters) in the city
- **P-1 (Priority road):** On the main avenues like Gran Vía and Avenida del Puerto
- **R-403 (Roundabout):** At the major glorietas where the Turia meets main roads
- **ZBE signs:** Valencia's growing Zona de Bajas Emisiones around the city centre

## ⚠️ US → Spain Differences for Regulatory Signs

| US Sign                                   | Spain Sign                                            | Key Difference                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Red octagon STOP                          | Red octagon STOP (R-2)                                | Same shape! But in Spain, 4-way stops virtually don't exist     |
| Yield (inverted triangle)                 | Ceda el paso (R-1)                                    | Same shape, same meaning                                        |
| Speed limit (white rectangle, black text) | Speed limit (red circle, white, black number) — R-301 | Different shape! US = rectangle. Spain = circle with RED border |
| No U-turn (black on white)                | R-304 (red circle, white)                             | Same meaning, different design                                  |
| Right turn on red allowed                 | Right turn on red **NEVER** allowed                   | This will trip you up every time!                               |
| Yellow diamond = warning                  | Yellow diamond = **PRIORITY ROAD** (P-1)              | Completely different meaning!                                   |
| No passing zone (yellow)                  | R-200 (red circle, two cars)                          | Different design, same concept                                  |
| Minimum speed rare                        | R-411 (blue circle, white number)                     | Much more common in Spain on motorways                          |
| School zone (yellow pentagon)             | No equivalent shape                                   | Spain uses warning triangle with children pictogram             |
| One-way (black arrow on white rectangle)  | R-101 (no entry) + S-30 (one-way info sign)           | Spain uses TWO signs — a prohibition at entry + info at start   |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **Red circle = PROHIBITION** (you CANNOT). **Blue circle = OBLIGATION** (you MUST).
2. **R-100** (empty circle) = no traffic either direction. **R-101** (red bar) = no entry from this side (one-way).
3. **R-307** = no stopping at all. **R-308** = no parking (brief stops OK).
4. **STOP (R-2)** = you MUST stop even if the road is empty. **YIELD (R-1)** = only stop if there's traffic.
5. **Right turn on red is NEVER allowed in Spain.** Period.
6. **Yellow diamond (P-1) = PRIORITY ROAD** in Europe — NOT a warning like in the US!
7. **Sign hierarchy:** Police → Temporary → Traffic lights → Vertical signs → Road markings.
8. **New for 2026:** VMP (patinete/scooter) signs added to both prohibition and obligation families.
9. **Speed limit signs are circles in Spain** (not rectangles like the US).
10. **Key verbs:** _prohibir_ (to prohibit), _obligar_ (to require), _ceder el paso_ (to yield).
