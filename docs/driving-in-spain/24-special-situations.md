# 24 — Special Situations / Situaciones Especiales

## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐⭐ (Core Exam Knowledge)

## 🎯 What This Topic Covers

This topic covers driving scenarios that require **specific knowledge** beyond normal rules: tunnels, level crossings, motorway breakdowns, school zones, low emission zones (ZBE), and sharing the road with vulnerable users. These are DGT exam favourites because they combine multiple rules into realistic scenarios.

## 🚇 TUNNELS — Túneles

Tunnels have their own set of rules because they're enclosed spaces with limited escape routes.

| Rule                    | Detail                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lights**              | Dipped headlights (cruce) ALWAYS — even if tunnel is well lit, even during the day                                                                    |
| **Sunglasses**          | Remove them BEFORE entering — your eyes need to adjust quickly                                                                                        |
| **Distance**            | Increase following distance — at least 100m at 100 km/h (some tunnels have markings)                                                                  |
| **Overtaking**          | Prohibited in tunnels unless there are 2+ lanes in your direction                                                                                     |
| **Stopping**            | Prohibited except in emergency — use designated lay-bys if available                                                                                  |
| **Speed**               | Obey posted limits — often reduced inside tunnels                                                                                                     |
| **Breakdown in tunnel** | Turn off engine, turn on hazard lights + V-16, exit vehicle on side AWAY from traffic, walk to nearest emergency exit                                 |
| **Fire in tunnel**      | Turn off engine, leave keys in ignition (so emergency services can move your car), exit via nearest emergency escape route. Do NOT try to reverse out |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — Fire in Tunnel

If there's a fire in a tunnel: **leave the keys in the ignition and walk to the nearest emergency exit.** Do NOT take the keys — firefighters need to move cars quickly. Do NOT try to drive or reverse out — you'll block other people.

### Tunnel Signs

| Sign                         | Meaning                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **S-5**                      | Tunnel ahead — lights compulsory |
| **S-5a**                     | Tunnel length indicator          |
| Green running person + arrow | Emergency exit                   |
| Fire extinguisher symbol     | Emergency equipment location     |
| SOS with telephone           | Emergency phone                  |

## 🚂 LEVEL CROSSINGS — Pasos a Nivel

Level crossings are where a road crosses a railway line at the same level. They're one of the most dangerous points on any road.

| Rule                                | Detail                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Red lights flashing alternately** | STOP — train approaching. Do NOT cross under any circumstances                                                                       |
| **Barriers lowering/lowered**       | STOP — even if you can't see a train                                                                                                 |
| **No barriers, no lights**          | LOOK both ways, LISTEN, cross only when sure it's safe                                                                               |
| **Stalled on the tracks**           | Get everyone out immediately. Try to push vehicle off. If impossible, RUN along the track (not perpendicular — debris flies forward) |
| **Never**                           | Race a train to the crossing, stop ON the tracks, overtake near a level crossing                                                     |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — Stalled on Tracks

If your car stalls on the tracks: **get everyone out and run ALONG the track in the direction the train is coming FROM** (so debris flies away from you, not toward you). Never run perpendicular.

## 🚗💨 MOTORWAY BREAKDOWNS — Averías en Autopista/Autovía

| Step | Action                                                     |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Move to the hard shoulder (arcén) as far right as possible |
| 2    | Turn on hazard lights                                      |
| 3    | Activate V-16 beacon on the roof                           |
| 4    | Put on reflective vest (inside the car!)                   |
| 5    | Exit on the side AWAY from traffic (passenger side)        |
| 6    | Move behind the crash barrier if there is one              |
| 7    | Call 112 or your assistance number                         |
| 8    | Stay AWAY from the vehicle — wait behind the barrier       |

### The Emergency Corridor — Carril de Emergencia (2026)

In traffic jams on motorways, drivers must create a clear path for emergency vehicles:

| Lanes       | What to Do                                                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **2 lanes** | Left lane moves LEFT, right lane moves RIGHT — corridor in the middle                      |
| **3 lanes** | Left lane moves LEFT, centre and right lanes move RIGHT — corridor between left and centre |

**Status (Feb 2026):** This rule is part of the Reglamento General de Circulación reform. The DGT has confirmed it will not take effect in early 2026 — the exact implementation date is still pending. Already common practice in much of Europe.

## 🏫 SCHOOL ZONES — Zonas Escolares

| Rule                   | Detail                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Warning sign**       | P-21 (triangle with children symbol)                                             |
| **Speed**              | Usually Zona 30 near schools — 30 km/h                                           |
| **Extra caution**      | Children are unpredictable — they may run into the road without looking          |
| **Stopped school bus** | No special "stop for school bus" rule like the US — but exercise extreme caution |
| **Double parking**     | PROHIBITED near schools, though sadly common — don't do it                       |

## 🟢 LOW EMISSION ZONES — Zonas de Bajas Emisiones (ZBE)

As of 2026, all Spanish cities with 50,000+ residents must have a ZBE. Valencia's is expanding.

| Badge    | Access                                     |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 🔵 CERO  | ✅ Full access everywhere                  |
| 🟢💙 ECO | ✅ Full access                             |
| 🟢 C     | ✅ Usually access (check municipality)     |
| 🟡 B     | ⚠️ Restricted — may be banned in some ZBEs |
| ❌ None  | 🚫 Banned from most ZBEs                   |

**Fine for entering without the right badge:** Up to €200

**Check your vehicle:** DGT website or miDGT app. Foreign-registered vehicles may need additional registration.

## 🚲 SHARING THE ROAD — Vulnerable Users

### Cyclists

| Rule                    | Detail                                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Lateral distance**    | Minimum **1.5 metres** when overtaking                                         |
| **Speed reduction**     | Reduce by **20 km/h** below the limit when passing (2026)                      |
| **Crossing solid line** | Permitted if needed for 1.5m distance (2026)                                   |
| **Group of cyclists**   | Once the first enters an intersection/roundabout, the whole group has priority |
| **Cycle lanes**         | Do NOT drive or park in them                                                   |

### VMP (Electric Scooters / Patinetes)

| Rule (2026)       | Detail                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Insurance**     | Mandatory (Ley 5/2025). VMP >25 kg **and** >14 km/h = motor vehicle, immediate obligation. Lighter VMP = required once DGT registry launches |
| **Maximum speed** | 25 km/h                                                                                                                                      |
| **Helmet**        | Mandatory in some municipalities (not yet national law)                                                                                      |
| **Pavements**     | PROHIBITED — must use road or cycle lane                                                                                                     |
| **Minimum age**   | 16 years                                                                                                                                     |
| **Lights**        | Must be on during riding                                                                                                                     |
| **New signs**     | R-116 (no entry for VMP) and R-416 (VMP path)                                                                                                |

### Pedestrians

- Always have priority at crossings
- Always have priority when you're turning into their street
- Absolute priority in S-220 (residential) and pedestrian zones
- Extra caution near schools, hospitals, elderly care homes

## 🗣️ Conjugation Spotlight

### DETENERSE — to stop (reflexive, e → ie)

|             | Presente      | Pretérito      | Imperfecto     |
| ----------- | ------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| yo          | me detengo    | me detuve      | me detenía     |
| tú          | te detienes   | te detuviste   | te detenías    |
| él/ella     | se detiene    | se detuvo      | se detenía     |
| nosotros    | nos detenemos | nos detuvimos  | nos deteníamos |
| vosotros    | os detenéis   | os detuvisteis | os deteníais   |
| ellos/ellas | se detienen   | se detuvieron  | se detenían    |

**Note:** Preterite is irregular — follows the _tener_ pattern (detuve, detuviste, detuvo...).

**Key sentences:**

- _Me detengo ante el paso a nivel cuando parpadean las luces._ — I stop at the level crossing when the lights flash.
- _Me detuve en el arcén porque tenía una avería._ — I stopped on the hard shoulder because I had a breakdown.

## ⚠️ US → Spain Differences

| US                                       | Spain                                                         | Key Difference                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| School bus: ALL traffic MUST stop        | No school bus stop rule                                       | Spain doesn't have this — watch for P-21 warning signs instead |
| Low emission zones rare                  | **ZBE mandatory** in cities 50K+ population                   | Badge system + fines                                           |
| No specific tunnel sunglasses rule       | **Remove sunglasses before entering** tunnels                 | Tested on DGT exam                                             |
| Tunnel fire: follow instructions         | Tunnel fire: **leave keys in ignition**, walk to exit         | Keys stay — so firefighters can move cars                      |
| Level crossing: stop if lights flash     | Level crossing: stop if lights flash                          | Same rule                                                      |
| No emergency corridor law in most states | **Emergency corridor** on motorways (2026)                    | Left moves left, right moves right                             |
| Electric scooters: varies wildly by city | VMP: national rules — insurance, speed limit 25 km/h, age 16+ | Spain has unified rules                                        |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **Tunnels: dipped headlights ALWAYS.** Remove sunglasses. No overtaking unless 2+ lanes.
2. **Tunnel fire: leave keys in ignition, walk to nearest emergency exit.** Never reverse.
3. **Level crossing red lights flashing: STOP.** Even if no train is visible.
4. **Stalled on tracks: evacuate, run ALONG the track** in the direction the train comes from.
5. **Motorway breakdown: arcén → hazard lights → V-16 → vest → exit passenger side → behind barrier.**
6. **Emergency corridor (2026):** Left lane left, right lane right.
7. **ZBE: check your car's badge.** No badge or wrong badge = up to €200 fine.
8. **Cyclists: 1.5m distance, reduce 20 km/h.** May cross solid line to achieve this.
9. **VMP (scooters): 25 km/h max, insurance mandatory (Ley 5/2025), no pavements.**
10. **Key verbs:** _detenerse_ (to stop), _evacuar_ (to evacuate), _respetar_ (to respect).
