# 12 — Manoeuvres / Maniobras

## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ (Core Exam Knowledge)

## 🎯 What This Topic Covers

Manoeuvres are the **actions** you perform while driving — overtaking, lane changes, turning, reversing, and U-turns. The DGT tests both **how** to perform them and **when they are prohibited**. Overtaking rules changed significantly in 2026, so pay close attention.

## 🚗 OVERTAKING — El Adelantamiento

### The Golden Rule

**In Spain, you ALWAYS overtake on the LEFT.** Overtaking on the right is prohibited except in very specific situations.

### The 5 Steps of a Safe Overtake

| Step  | Action                                                    | Spanish                                      |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | **Check** — mirrors + blind spot                          | Comprobar retrovisores y ángulo muerto       |
| **2** | **Signal** — left indicator                               | Señalizar con el intermitente izquierdo      |
| **3** | **Move** — into the left lane when safe                   | Desplazarse al carril izquierdo              |
| **4** | **Pass** — overtake without exceeding the speed limit     | Adelantar sin superar el límite de velocidad |
| **5** | **Return** — signal right, return to your lane when clear | Señalizar derecha, volver al carril propio   |

### ⚠️ 2026 RULE CHANGE — Overtaking Cyclists & Stopped Vehicles

**Status (Feb 2026):** This rule is part of the Reglamento General de Circulación reform draft. It is expected to become law during 2026 but has not yet been formally enacted. Check DGT for the latest status.

When overtaking a cyclist or a vehicle stopped on the hard shoulder:

| Requirement             | Detail                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Speed reduction**     | Reduce speed by at least **20 km/h** below the road's speed limit                          |
| **Lateral distance**    | Maintain at least **1.5 metres** of lateral space                                          |
| **Crossing solid line** | You MAY cross a solid white line if needed to maintain the 1.5m distance, BUT only if safe |

**Example:** On a 90 km/h road → you must slow to at most **70 km/h** when passing a cyclist or stopped vehicle.

**💡 Mick's Memory Trick:** "Drop 20, give 1.5" — reduce by 20 km/h, give 1.5 metres.

### When Is Overtaking PROHIBITED?

| Situation                                                  | Why It's Prohibited                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🚫 **No-overtaking sign (R-200)** active                   | Sign says no!                                                                                               |
| 🚫 **Solid white line** on your side                       | You cannot cross it (EXCEPT for cyclists/stopped vehicles per 2026 rule)                                    |
| 🚫 **Insufficient visibility** — bends, hills, tunnels     | You can't see oncoming traffic                                                                              |
| 🚫 **At or near a pedestrian crossing**                    | Pedestrians may step out                                                                                    |
| 🚫 **At level crossings** (pasos a nivel)                  | Too dangerous                                                                                               |
| 🚫 **At intersections** (unless you're on a priority road) | Vehicles may enter                                                                                          |
| 🚫 **In bad weather** when visibility is very poor         | Too risky                                                                                                   |
| 🚫 **On motorways in snow/ice** (2026 rule)                | Prohibited — must stay in right lane for emergency access (lane discipline rule, part of Reglamento reform) |

### When Can You Overtake on the RIGHT?

Overtaking on the right is ONLY permitted:

1. When the vehicle ahead is **signalling a left turn** and has moved to the left — you may pass on their right
2. In urban areas with **separate lanes** — each lane may move at its own speed (this is normal lane flowing, not "overtaking" per se)
3. On motorways when traffic is flowing in **multiple lanes** — this is lane movement, not formal overtaking

## 🔄 LANE CHANGES — Cambio de Carril

### The Procedure

| Step | Action                                                        |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | **Check** mirrors (interior + exterior)                       |
| 2    | **Check** blind spot (ángulo muerto) — turn your head briefly |
| 3    | **Signal** with indicator — with enough advance time          |
| 4    | **Move** smoothly into the new lane when safe                 |
| 5    | **Cancel** indicator after completing the change              |

### Key Rules

- The vehicle in the lane you're moving **INTO** has priority
- If two vehicles want to enter the same lane, the one **closer** to that lane goes first
- You MUST signal, even if you think no one is around
- On motorways, stay in the **rightmost free lane** — the left lane is for overtaking only

## ↩️ TURNING — Girar

### Turning Right (Girar a la Derecha)

1. Move to the **rightmost lane** well in advance
2. Signal right
3. Yield to pedestrians crossing the street you're turning into
4. Make the turn keeping as close to the right edge as possible

### Turning Left (Girar a la Izquierda)

1. Move to the **leftmost lane** (or centre of the road on two-lane roads)
2. Signal left
3. Yield to oncoming traffic
4. Yield to pedestrians crossing the street you're turning into
5. Complete the turn into the corresponding lane

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — Pedestrians When Turning

Whenever you turn into another street, **pedestrians crossing that street ALWAYS have priority** — even without a marked crossing. This was covered in Page 08 (priority rules) and the DGT tests it repeatedly.

## 🔄 U-TURNS — Cambio de Sentido

### When Is a U-Turn Permitted?

U-turns are allowed ONLY when:

- There is **no sign prohibiting it** (R-304)
- There is **no solid line** preventing it
- You have **sufficient visibility** in both directions
- It doesn't obstruct or endanger other traffic
- You're NOT on a motorway/autovía (use the designated turnarounds instead)

### When Is a U-Turn PROHIBITED?

| Situation                                              | Spanish                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| 🚫 Sign R-304 present                                  | Cambio de sentido prohibido   |
| 🚫 Sign R-303 (no left turn — also prohibits U-turns!) | Giro a la izquierda prohibido |
| 🚫 On motorways/autovías                               | Autopistas y autovías         |
| 🚫 Near bends, hills, tunnels with no visibility       | Falta de visibilidad          |
| 🚫 Near intersections or level crossings               | Intersecciones, pasos a nivel |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — R-303 Also Prohibits U-Turns

The "no left turn" sign (R-303) **also prohibits U-turns**. The DGT loves testing this! If you can't turn left, you certainly can't do a full U-turn.

## ⏪ REVERSING — Marcha Atrás

Reversing is considered a **dangerous manoeuvre** and should be avoided whenever possible.

### Rules for Reversing

| Rule                 | Detail                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Maximum distance** | 15 metres (or the minimum necessary for the manoeuvre)        |
| **Speed**            | Very slow — walking pace                                      |
| **Prohibited on**    | Motorways and autovías — NEVER reverse                        |
| **Prohibited on**    | Roads where it would be dangerous or obstruct traffic         |
| **Helper**           | Use a guide (person directing you) when visibility is limited |
| **One-way streets**  | You may reverse if necessary, up to 15 metres                 |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — Reversing on Motorways

Reversing on a motorway or autovía is **ALWAYS prohibited** — even if you miss your exit. If you miss an exit, continue to the next one. Never reverse or do a U-turn on a motorway.

A regular -ar verb, but arguably the most important verb in this topic.

| él/ella     | adelanta    | adelantó      | adelantaba    |

- _No adelanté porque no había visibilidad._ — I didn't overtake because there was no visibility.

## ⚠️ US → Spain Differences for Manoeuvres

| US                                           | Spain                                                                     | Key Difference                                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Pass on left OR right on multi-lane highways | Overtake on the **LEFT only** (right only in specific exceptions)         | Spain is much stricter about left-only overtaking |
| No specific cyclist overtaking speed rule    | Must reduce by **20 km/h** and give **1.5m** (2026)                       | Brand new rule with teeth                         |
| May cross solid yellow to turn left          | May cross solid white line ONLY for cyclist/stopped vehicle safety (2026) | Normally solid = never cross                      |
| U-turn usually allowed unless signed         | U-turn only where specifically safe and allowed                           | Spain is more restrictive                         |
| Reversing on highway shoulder sometimes seen | Reversing on motorway **ALWAYS prohibited**                               | Absolutely never in Spain                         |
| Right on red allowed                         | Right on red **NEVER** allowed                                            | As always!                                        |
| No left turn sign = no left turn only        | No left turn sign (R-303) = no left turn AND no U-turn                    | Broader prohibition in Spain                      |
| Blind spot check: recommended                | Blind spot check: **mandatory** for exam                                  | DGT practical exam fails for not checking         |
| Speed can increase to overtake (some states) | Speed limit is ABSOLUTE — you CANNOT exceed it to overtake (2022+ rule)   | Major change — no more +20 km/h overtaking margin |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **Always overtake on the LEFT.** Right-side overtaking only in specific exceptions.
2. **2026 rule: "Drop 20, give 1.5"** — reduce speed by 20 km/h and maintain 1.5m when passing cyclists or stopped vehicles.
3. **2026: You MAY cross a solid line** to maintain 1.5m from a cyclist/stopped vehicle IF safe.
4. **No overtaking** at pedestrian crossings, level crossings, tunnels, bends with no visibility, or when R-200 is active.
5. **R-303 (no left turn) ALSO prohibits U-turns.**
6. **Reversing: maximum 15 metres.** NEVER on motorways.
7. **You CANNOT exceed the speed limit to overtake.** The +20 km/h margin was removed in 2022.
8. **Always check blind spot** before lane changes — the practical exam fails for this.
9. **When turning into a street, yield to ALL pedestrians** crossing that street.
10. **Key verbs:** _adelantar_ (to overtake), _señalizar_ (to signal), _girar_ (to turn), _rebasar_ (to pass).
