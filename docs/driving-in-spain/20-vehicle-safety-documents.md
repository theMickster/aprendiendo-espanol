# 20 — Vehicle Safety & Documents / Seguridad del Vehículo y Documentación

## ⭐ Difficulty: ⭐⭐ (Core Exam Knowledge)

## 🎯 What This Topic Covers

This topic covers what your vehicle must carry, the documents you need, the ITV inspection (Spain's equivalent of the MOT), tyres, seatbelts, child seats, and the new 2026 V-16 beacon. It's one of the most practical DGT topics — you'll use this knowledge every day, not just on the exam.

## 📋 DOCUMENTS YOU MUST CARRY

When driving in Spain, you must always have these documents available:

| Document                  | Spanish                                  | Notes                                                      |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Driving licence**       | Permiso de conducir / Carnet de conducir | Your physical card (or via miDGT app for Spanish licences) |
| **Vehicle registration**  | Permiso de circulación                   | The "logbook" — proves the car is legally registered       |
| **Technical data sheet**  | Ficha técnica / Tarjeta de ITV           | Contains vehicle specifications and ITV stamp              |
| **Insurance certificate** | Certificado de seguro                    | Proof of valid, current insurance                          |
| **ITV sticker**           | Pegatina de ITV                          | Displayed on windscreen — shows inspection expiry          |
| **ID / Passport / NIE**   | DNI / Pasaporte / NIE                    | Personal identification                                    |

**💡 Tip:** The **miDGT app** lets you carry digital versions of your Spanish driving licence and vehicle documents. However, always keep physical copies in the car as backup.

## 🔧 THE ITV — Spain's Vehicle Inspection

The ITV is Spain's mandatory roadworthiness test. Without it, you cannot legally drive.

### ITV Frequency

| Vehicle Age       | Frequency         |
| ----------------- | ----------------- |
| **New → 4 years** | No ITV needed     |
| **4–10 years**    | Every **2 years** |
| **10+ years**     | Every **year**    |

### What the ITV Checks

| Area                       | Spanish              | What They Check                                         |
| -------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔦 **Lights**              | Alumbrado            | All lights working, properly aligned                    |
| 🛞 **Tyres**               | Neumáticos           | Tread depth ≥ 1.6mm, no damage, correct pressure        |
| 🛑 **Brakes**              | Frenos               | Pads, discs, brake fluid, handbrake                     |
| 💨 **Emissions**           | Emisiones            | Exhaust gases within legal limits                       |
| 🪞 **Mirrors**             | Retrovisores         | All present and properly adjusted                       |
| 🔊 **Horn**                | Claxon               | Working                                                 |
| 🏷️ **Number plates**       | Matrícula            | Clean, visible, undamaged                               |
| 🔧 **Suspension/steering** | Suspensión/dirección | No excessive play, shock absorbers OK                   |
| 🪟 **Windscreen**          | Parabrisas           | No cracks in the driver's line of sight, wipers working |

### 2026 ITV Crackdown

The DGT has tightened ITV enforcement in 2026: driving without a valid ITV now results in a **€200 fine + 3 points** deducted. If expired more than 12 months: **€500 fine**. Officers may **immobilise your vehicle on the spot**. The informal "grace period" no longer exists.

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — ITV Frequency

"When does a new car need its first ITV?" — **After 4 years.** Not 3, not 5. Four years. Then every 2 years until year 10, then annually.

## 🧰 MANDATORY VEHICLE EQUIPMENT (2026)

| Item                             | Spanish                               | Notes                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ✅ **V-16 connected beacon**     | Baliza V-16 conectada                 | **Mandatory from 1 Jan 2026.** Replaces emergency triangles. Store in glove compartment.  |
| ✅ **Reflective vest**           | Chaleco reflectante                   | At least 1 — must be inside the cabin (not the boot!) so you can put it on before exiting |
| ✅ **Spare wheel OR repair kit** | Rueda de repuesto o kit antipinchazos | Most modern cars come with a repair kit instead of a full spare                           |
| ✅ **Insurance documentation**   | Documentación del seguro              | Must be current and available                                                             |
| ⚠️ **Emergency triangles**       | Triángulos de emergencia              | No longer mandatory in Spain as of 2026, but keep them for driving in other EU countries  |

### 🚨 DGT Exam Trap — Reflective Vest Location

The vest must be **inside the vehicle cabin** (usually under the seat or in a door pocket) — NOT in the boot. Why? Because you need to put it on BEFORE stepping out of the car. If it's in the boot, you'd have to walk outside unprotected to get it.

## 🛞 TYRES — Los Neumáticos

| Rule                    | Detail                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Minimum tread depth** | **1.6 mm** (the legal minimum — DGT recommends replacing at 3mm)             |
| **Mixing tyres**        | Same type on each axle — don't mix radial and cross-ply on the same axle     |
| **Pressure**            | Check monthly and before long trips — refer to manufacturer's door sticker   |
| **Snow chains**         | Mandatory when sign R-412 is displayed. Fit to drive wheels.                 |
| **Winter tyres**        | Not mandatory in Spain generally, but required when sign R-412b is displayed |

## 💺 SEATBELTS & CHILD SEATS

### Seatbelt Rules

| Rule                     | Detail                                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Front seats**          | Seatbelt **ALWAYS** compulsory — driver + front passenger                                          |
| **Rear seats**           | Seatbelt **ALWAYS** compulsory — all passengers                                                    |
| **Fine for not wearing** | €200 + 4 points                                                                                    |
| **Pregnant women**       | Must wear seatbelt (with special adaptor if needed)                                                |
| **Exemptions**           | Very limited — some professional drivers in specific circumstances. Being discussed in 2026 reform |

### Child Seat Rules (SRI — Sistemas de Retención Infantil)

| Rule                  | Detail                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mandatory until**   | Child reaches **1.35 metres** height (roughly age 12)                                                                       |
| **Recommended until** | **1.50 metres** — the seatbelt doesn't fit properly until this height                                                       |
| **Where**             | **Rear seats** — children under 1.35m must sit in the back                                                                  |
| **Exception**         | Front seat allowed ONLY if rear seats are already occupied by other children in car seats, or the vehicle has no rear seats |
| **Seat standards**    | Must comply with ECE R44/04 or i-Size (R129)                                                                                |
| **Fine**              | €200 + 4 points for not using proper child restraint                                                                        |

## 🟢 ENVIRONMENTAL BADGE — Distintivo Ambiental

Spain's DGT issues environmental badges based on vehicle emissions:

| Badge        | Spanish        | Vehicles                                         | ZBE Access                 |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| 🔵 **CERO**  | Cero emisiones | Electric, hydrogen, plug-in hybrid (≥40km range) | ✅ Full access             |
| 🟢💙 **ECO** | Eco            | Hybrid, CNG, LPG vehicles                        | ✅ Full access             |
| 🟢 **C**     | C              | Petrol from 2006+, diesel from 2014+             | ✅ Usually access          |
| 🟡 **B**     | B              | Petrol from 2001+, diesel from 2006+             | ⚠️ Restricted in some ZBEs |
| ❌ **None**  | Sin distintivo | Older vehicles                                   | 🚫 Banned from most ZBEs   |

**Valencia's ZBE** is expanding. Check your vehicle's badge at the DGT website or miDGT app. Without the right badge, you cannot enter the restricted zone — fine: €200.

| ----------- | -------- | ---------- | ---------- |

**Key sentences:**

- _Siempre llevo el chaleco reflectante en el coche._ — I always carry the reflective vest in the car.
- _Hay que llevar el cinturón de seguridad._ — You have to wear the seatbelt.

|             | Presente  | Pretérito   | Imperfecto  |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------- | ----------- |

## 🏙️ Valencia Connection

- **ITV stations near Valencia:** Several in the metropolitan area — book via www.itv.gva.es
- **ZBE Valencia:** Expanding in the city centre — check if your car has the right badge before driving into the restricted zone
- **Environmental badge:** Check at sede.dgt.gob.es or miDGT app. Foreign-registered cars may need separate registration
- **Insurance:** Mandatory third-party minimum. Shop around — Valencia has competitive rates
- **Reflective vest:** Keep one in the car at all times — Valencia police enforce this during routine checks
- **V-16 beacon:** Available at major retailers (Carrefour, Amazon.es, auto parts shops) — buy a DGT-approved model only (check list at dgt.es/v16)
- **Child seats:** Strictly enforced — you'll see police checking at school zones near Penya-Roja
- **Tyres:** Mediterranean climate means you rarely need winter tyres in Valencia, but check if driving to the mountains (ski resorts in Teruel/Aragon)

## ⚠️ US → Spain Differences

| US                                           | Spain                                                            | Key Difference                          |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| State inspection varies                      | **ITV mandatory** nationwide — strictly enforced                 | No flexibility in Spain                 |
| Grace period common                          | **No grace period** as of 2026 — expired = fine + immobilisation | Major 2026 change                       |
| Emergency triangles                          | **V-16 connected beacon** (2026)                                 | Different equipment requirement         |
| Child seat until varies by state             | Child seat until **1.35m height** (rear seats)                   | Height-based, not age-based             |
| Seatbelt laws vary by state                  | Seatbelt **ALWAYS mandatory** — all seats                        | No exceptions for rear seats            |
| No environmental badge system in most states | **Distintivo ambiental** required for ZBEs                       | Check your car before driving in cities |

| State registration + plates                  | Permiso de circulación + Spanish matrícula                       | Similar concept                         |
| Minimum tyre tread: 2/32" (~1.6mm)           | Minimum tyre tread: **1.6mm**                                    | Same standard!                          |
| Reflective vest: not required                | Reflective vest: **mandatory in cabin**                          | Keep it under the seat, not in the boot |

## 📌 Summary — What You Need to Remember

1. **Always carry:** driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, ITV sticker, ID.
2. **ITV schedule:** First at 4 years. Then every 2 years (4–10). Then yearly (10+).
3. **2026 ITV crackdown:** No grace period. Expired = €200 fine + 3 points. 12+ months expired = €500. Vehicle may be immobilised.
4. **Mandatory equipment (2026):** V-16 connected beacon (glove compartment), reflective vest (cabin — NOT boot), spare/repair kit.
5. **Reflective vest goes IN THE CABIN** — not the boot. Put it on BEFORE exiting.
6. **Minimum tyre tread: 1.6mm.** Check pressure monthly.
7. **Seatbelts mandatory for ALL seats.** Fine: €200 + 4 points.
8. **Child seats until 1.35m height.** Children must sit in the rear.
9. **Environmental badge (distintivo ambiental)** needed for ZBEs — check your car!
10. **Key verbs:** _llevar_ (to carry/wear), _revisar_ (to check), _pasar la ITV_ (to pass the inspection).
