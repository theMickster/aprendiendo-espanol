# ✅ The Present Perfect (Pretérito Perfecto)

## What Have You Done Today? Spain's Favorite Past Tense!

**⏱️ Study Time: 15-20 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Talk about recent past actions using haber + past participle -- the way they do it in Spain!**

---

## Overview

The present perfect (pretérito perfecto) is one of the most important tenses to learn in Spain. While Latin America often uses the preterite for recent actions, Spain uses the present perfect constantly in daily conversation. Living in Valencia, you'll hear this tense dozens of times a day.

**English equivalent:** "I have eaten," "She has gone," "We have seen"

---

## 1️⃣ Formation: HABER + Past Participle

The present perfect is formed with two parts:

1. The auxiliary verb **haber** (conjugated)
2. The **past participle** of the main verb (never changes form!)

### Conjugation of HABER

| Person              | Spanish | English              |
| ------------------- | ------- | -------------------- |
| Yo                  | he      | I have               |
| Tú                  | has     | you have             |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | ha      | he/she has, you have |
| Nosotros/as         | hemos   | we have              |
| Vosotros/as         | habéis  | you all have         |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | han     | they/you all have    |

---

## 2️⃣ Regular Past Participles

The past participle is easy to form for regular verbs:

| Verb type | Rule           | Example              |
| --------- | -------------- | -------------------- |
| -ar verbs | -ar → **-ado** | hablar → habl**ado** |
| -er verbs | -er → **-ido** | comer → com**ido**   |
| -ir verbs | -ir → **-ido** | vivir → viv**ido**   |

**Examples:**

1. **He hablado con mi madre hoy**

   - (I have spoken with my mother today)

2. **¿Has comido ya?**

   - (Have you eaten already?)

3. **Hemos vivido aquí dos años**
   - (We have lived here two years)

---

## 3️⃣ Irregular Past Participles

These must be memorized -- there's no shortcut!

| Infinitive | Past Participle | Meaning         |
| ---------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| hacer      | **hecho**       | done/made       |
| decir      | **dicho**       | said/told       |
| escribir   | **escrito**     | written         |
| ver        | **visto**       | seen            |
| poner      | **puesto**      | put/placed      |
| abrir      | **abierto**     | opened          |
| volver     | **vuelto**      | returned        |
| romper     | **roto**        | broken          |
| morir      | **muerto**      | died            |
| cubrir     | **cubierto**    | covered         |
| descubrir  | **descubierto** | discovered      |
| resolver   | **resuelto**    | resolved/solved |

4. **He hecho los deberes**

   - (I have done the homework)

5. **¿Has visto la película?**

   - (Have you seen the movie?)

6. **Ha escrito tres correos**

   - (He/She has written three emails)

7. **Hemos descubierto un restaurante increíble**
   - (We have discovered an incredible restaurant)

---

## ⚠️ Important Rule: Nothing Goes Between HABER and the Participle!

In Spanish, NOTHING can separate haber from the past participle. Not "no," not "ya," not any other word.

| Wrong                    | Right                                  |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| ~~He todavía no comido~~ | **Todavía no he comido**               |
| ~~No he nunca ido~~      | **Nunca he ido** / **No he ido nunca** |
| ~~Has ya terminado?~~    | **¿Ya has terminado?**                 |

The negation "no" and other words go BEFORE haber:

8. **No he comido todavía**

   - (I haven't eaten yet)

9. **Ya hemos llegado**
   - (We have already arrived)

---

## 4️⃣ Spain vs Latin America: Why This Matters for You

Since your family lives in Spain, this is crucial:

| Situation         | Spain (present perfect)       | Latin America (preterite) |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Today's breakfast | **Hoy he comido paella**      | Hoy comí paella           |
| This morning      | **Esta mañana he ido al gym** | Esta mañana fui al gym    |
| Just now          | **He terminado ahora mismo**  | Terminé ahora mismo       |
| Already done      | **Ya he hecho la compra**     | Ya hice la compra         |

In Spain, if it happened **today** or in a time period that's still ongoing (this week, this month, this year), you use the present perfect. Learn the Spanish way -- it's what you'll hear every day in Valencia!

---

## 5️⃣ Key Trigger Words

These words signal present perfect in Spain:

| Spanish             | English    | Example                               |
| ------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------- |
| ya                  | already    | **Ya he comido**                      |
| todavía no / aún no | not yet    | **Todavía no he ido**                 |
| alguna vez          | ever       | **¿Has estado alguna vez en Madrid?** |
| nunca               | never      | **Nunca he probado eso**              |
| hoy                 | today      | **Hoy he trabajado mucho**            |
| esta semana         | this week  | **Esta semana hemos ido al cine**     |
| este mes            | this month | **Este mes ha llovido mucho**         |
| este año            | this year  | **Este año hemos viajado bastante**   |

---

## 6️⃣ Present Perfect vs Preterite: When to Use Which

| Present Perfect (he + participle)         | Preterite (fui, comí, etc.)               |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| No specific time / life experience        | Specific completed time in the past       |
| Time period still open (today, this week) | Time period closed (yesterday, last week) |
| "Have you ever...?"                       | "Did you...on Tuesday?"                   |

10. **He estado en Barcelona** (at some point in my life -- experience)
    vs **Estuve en Barcelona en enero** (specific time -- January)

11. **Hoy he comido bien** (today is still ongoing)
    vs **Ayer comí bien** (yesterday is over)

12. **Este año hemos viajado mucho** (this year is still going)
    vs **El año pasado viajamos mucho** (last year is finished)

## 🏖️ Valencia Daily Life Examples

13. **¿Habéis ido a la playa hoy?**

    - (Have you all gone to the beach today?)

14. **He probado la horchata por primera vez**

    - (I've tried horchata for the first time)

15. **Esta semana hemos hablado mucho español en clase**

    - (This week we've spoken a lot of Spanish in class)

16. **¿Has visto las Fallas alguna vez?**

    - (Have you ever seen Las Fallas?)

17. **Todavía no he aprendido valenciano**
    - (I haven't learned Valencian yet)

## 💡 Cultural Note

In Valencia (and all of Spain), you'll notice people use the present perfect far more than you might expect from textbooks. A waiter might ask "¿Habéis decidido ya?" (Have you decided yet?) and a friend will say "He quedado con María" (I've arranged to meet María). Embrace this -- it's how Spanish sounds in Spain!
