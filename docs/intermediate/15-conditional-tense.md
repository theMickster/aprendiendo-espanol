# 🤔 The Conditional Tense (El Condicional)

## What Would You Do? Polite Requests, Dreams, and Hypotheticals!

**⏱️ Study Time: 15-20 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Use the conditional tense for polite requests, hypothetical situations, and speculation!**

---

## Overview

The conditional tense is your key to sounding polite and sophisticated in Spanish. It's the equivalent of "would" in English: "I **would** go," "She **would** like," "We **would** have." You'll use it every day for ordering politely, giving advice, and imagining possibilities.

---

## 1️⃣ Formation: Infinitive + Conditional Endings

Great news: the conditional uses the **same endings for ALL verb types** (-ar, -er, -ir)! Just add the endings to the full infinitive.

### Conditional Endings

| Person              | Ending |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Yo                  | -ía    |
| Tú                  | -ías   |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | -ía    |
| Nosotros/as         | -íamos |
| Vosotros/as         | -íais  |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | -ían   |

**Notice:** These are the same endings as the imperfect of -er/-ir verbs! But they attach to the full infinitive.

---

## 2️⃣ Regular Conjugation Example: HABLAR

| Person              | Spanish     | English             |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ------------------- |
| Yo                  | hablaría    | I would speak       |
| Tú                  | hablarías   | you would speak     |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | hablaría    | he/she would speak  |
| Nosotros/as         | hablaríamos | we would speak      |
| Vosotros/as         | hablaríais  | you all would speak |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | hablarían   | they would speak    |

The same pattern works for -er and -ir verbs:

- comer → comería, comerías, comería, comeríamos, comeríais, comerían
- vivir → viviría, vivirías, viviría, viviríamos, viviríais, vivirían

---

## 3️⃣ Irregular Stems

The conditional has the **exact same irregular stems as the future tense** (see [Lesson 02](02-building-block-verbs-future-tense.md)). If you learned the future irregulars, you already know these!

| Infinitive | Irregular Stem | Conditional (yo) | Future (yo) |
| ---------- | -------------- | ---------------- | ----------- |
| tener      | tendr-         | tendría          | tendré      |
| hacer      | har-           | haría            | haré        |
| haber      | habr-          | habría           | habrá       |
| poder      | podr-          | podría           | podré       |
| saber      | sabr-          | sabría           | sabré       |
| poner      | pondr-         | pondría          | pondré      |
| salir      | saldr-         | saldría          | saldré      |
| venir      | vendr-         | vendría          | vendré      |
| querer     | querr-         | querría          | querré      |
| decir      | dir-           | diría            | diré        |
| caber      | cabr-          | cabría           | cabré       |
| valer      | valdr-         | valdría          | valdré      |

**Memory tip:** The stems change in the same way -- just swap the future endings (-é, -ás, -á...) for the conditional endings (-ía, -ías, -ía...).

## 4️⃣ Uses of the Conditional

### Polite Requests

The conditional makes requests sound much more courteous than the present tense.

1. **¿Podrías ayudarme?**

   - (Could you help me?)

2. **Me gustaría un café con leche**

   - (I would like a coffee with milk)

3. **¿Podría ver el menú?**

   - (Could I see the menu?)

4. **¿Sería posible cambiar la mesa?**
   - (Would it be possible to change the table?)

### Hypotheticals

5. **Yo iría, pero no puedo**

   - (I would go, but I can't)

6. **Compraríamos una casa en la playa, pero es muy cara**

   - (We would buy a house on the beach, but it's very expensive)

7. **Estudiaría más, pero estoy cansado**
   - (I would study more, but I'm tired)

### Advice

8. **Yo que tú, estudiaría más**

   - (If I were you, I'd study more)

9. **Yo en tu lugar, no diría nada**

   - (In your place, I wouldn't say anything)

10. **Deberías practicar cada día**
    - (You should practice every day)

### Speculation About the Past

11. **Serían las tres cuando llegó**

    - (It must have been 3 o'clock when he arrived)

12. **Tendría unos veinte años**
    - (He/She was probably around twenty years old)

### Future in the Past

13. **Dijo que vendría**

    - (He said he would come)

14. **Prometió que lo haría**

    - (He promised he would do it)

15. **Sabía que sería difícil**
    - (I knew it would be difficult)

## 🏖️ Valencia Daily Life Examples

### Ordering politely at a cafe:

16. **Querría una horchata, por favor**

    - (I would like a horchata, please)

17. **¿Me pondría un cortado?**
    - (Would you get me a cortado?)

### Asking for help:

18. **¿Podrías decirme dónde está el Mercado Central?**

    - (Could you tell me where the Central Market is?)

19. **¿Sabría decirme a qué hora cierra?**
    - (Would you happen to know what time it closes?)

### Imagining your dream home in El Carmen:

20. **Viviría en un piso en El Carmen con terraza**

    - (I would live in a flat in El Carmen with a terrace)

21. **Tendría vistas a la catedral**

    - (It would have views of the cathedral)

22. **Iría al Mercado Central cada mañana**
    - (I would go to the Central Market every morning)

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

| Mistake                                     | Problem                                                              | Correct                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ~~Yo irá mañana~~                           | Confusing future (-á) with conditional (-ía)                         | **Yo iría** (I would go) or **Yo iré** (I will go)                            |
| ~~Comía una paella~~ (meaning "would eat")  | Imperfect and conditional both end in -ía, but imperfect = "used to" | **Comería** una paella (I would eat) vs **Comía** una paella (I used to eat)  |
| ~~Yo quería un café~~ (for polite ordering) | This works too! But conditional is more formal                       | **Querría un café** (most polite) or **Quería un café** (common and accepted) |

**Note:** In everyday speech, many Spaniards use the imperfect (quería, podía) instead of the conditional for polite requests. Both are fine! But the conditional is the "textbook" polite form.
