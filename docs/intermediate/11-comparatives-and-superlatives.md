# ⚖️ Comparatives and Superlatives

## Comparing Things in Spanish: More, Less, Equal, and The Most!

**Level:** Intermediate (B1)
**Estimated Time:** 10-15 minutes
**Topics:** Inequality comparisons, equality comparisons, irregular comparatives, superlatives

---

## Overview

Whether you're comparing Valencia's barrios, debating which paella is best, or saying your daughter is as tall as her friend, you need comparatives and superlatives. Spanish has clear patterns for saying "more than," "less than," "as much as," and "the most."

## Learning Objectives

- Form inequality comparisons with más...que and menos...que
- Form equality comparisons with tan...como and tanto...como
- Use irregular comparatives (mejor, peor, mayor, menor)
- Form superlatives with el más...de and the -ísimo suffix

---

## 📈 Inequality Comparisons (More/Less Than)

### MÁS + adjective/adverb/noun + QUE (more...than)

1. **Valencia es más bonita que Madrid** (Valencia is more beautiful than Madrid)
2. **Julio es más caluroso que enero** (July is hotter than January)
3. **Mi hija mayor corre más rápido que yo** (My older daughter runs faster than me)
4. **Tengo más libros que mi hermano** (I have more books than my brother)

### MENOS + adjective/adverb/noun + QUE (less...than)

5. **Enero es menos caluroso que julio** (January is less hot than July)
6. **El autobús es menos rápido que el metro** (The bus is less fast than the metro)
7. **Hoy hay menos gente que ayer** (Today there are fewer people than yesterday)
8. **Olympia era menos grande que Valencia** (Olympia was less big than Valencia)

---

## ⚖️ Equality Comparisons (As...As)

### TAN + adjective/adverb + COMO (as...as)

Use **tan** before adjectives and adverbs:

1. **Ella es tan alta como su hermano** (She is as tall as her brother)
2. **El Turia es tan bonito como la playa** (The Turia is as beautiful as the beach)
3. **Corro tan rápido como puedo** (I run as fast as I can)

### TANTO/A/OS/AS + noun + COMO (as many/much...as)

Use **tanto** before nouns -- it must agree in gender and number:

| Form   | Use With                 | Example                                       |
| ------ | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| tanto  | masculine singular nouns | tanto tiempo como tú (as much time as you)    |
| tanta  | feminine singular nouns  | tanta paciencia como ella (as much patience)  |
| tantos | masculine plural nouns   | tantos libros como tú (as many books as you)  |
| tantas | feminine plural nouns    | tantas playas como Alicante (as many beaches) |

4. **Hoy no hace tanto calor como ayer** (Today it's not as hot as yesterday)
5. **Tengo tantos libros como tú** (I have as many books as you)
6. **Valencia tiene tantas fiestas como Barcelona** (Valencia has as many festivals as Barcelona)
7. **No tengo tanto dinero como él** (I don't have as much money as him)

### Verb + TANTO COMO (as much as)

Use **tanto como** after verbs (tanto doesn't change here):

8. **Estudio tanto como ella** (I study as much as her)
9. **Mi hija come tanto como yo** (My daughter eats as much as me)
10. **No llueve tanto como en Olympia** (It doesn't rain as much as in Olympia)

## 🔀 Irregular Comparatives

These adjectives have special comparative forms -- don't use "más" with them:

| Adjective | Comparative | Example                                 |
| --------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------- |
| bueno     | **mejor**   | Esta paella es **mejor** que la de ayer |
| malo      | **peor**    | Mi español es **peor** que mi inglés    |
| grande    | **mayor**   | Mi hija **mayor** tiene diez años       |
| pequeño   | **menor**   | Mi hija **menor** tiene siete años      |

**Important distinctions:**

- **mayor/menor** = older/younger (age) or bigger/smaller (abstract importance)
- **más grande/más pequeño** = physically bigger/smaller (size)

**Examples:**

1. **Mi hermano es mayor que yo** (My brother is older than me)
2. **Mi casa en Valencia es más grande que la de Olympia** (My house in Valencia is bigger than the one in Olympia -- physical size)
3. **La paella del domingo fue mejor que la del sábado** (Sunday's paella was better than Saturday's)
4. **El tráfico hoy es peor que ayer** (Traffic today is worse than yesterday)

**Never say:** ~~más bueno, más malo~~ (for comparisons)

## 🏆 Superlatives

### Relative Superlative: The Most/Least...of

**el/la/los/las + más/menos + adjective + de**

1. **Es el restaurante más barato de Valencia** (It's the cheapest restaurant in Valencia)
2. **La Malvarrosa es la playa más conocida de la ciudad** (Malvarrosa is the best-known beach in the city)
3. **Es la calle menos ruidosa del barrio** (It's the least noisy street in the neighborhood)
4. **Son los mejores churros de Valencia** (They are the best churros in Valencia)

**Note:** Use **de** (not "en") after superlatives -- "the best in Valencia" = "el mejor **de** Valencia"

### Absolute Superlative: -ÍSIMO/A (Extremely!)

Add **-ísimo/a/os/as** to make an adjective mean "extremely" or "very very":

| Adjective | Superlative      | Meaning                |
| --------- | ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| bueno     | buen**ísimo/a**  | extremely good         |
| malo      | mal**ísimo/a**   | extremely bad          |
| grande    | grand**ísimo/a** | extremely big          |
| guapo     | guap**ísimo/a**  | extremely good-looking |

**Spelling changes to watch for:**

| Adjective | Superlative     | Why                  |
| --------- | --------------- | -------------------- |
| rico      | riqu**ísimo**   | c → qu before -ísimo |
| largo     | largu**ísimo**  | g → gu before -ísimo |
| feliz     | felic**ísimo**  | z → c before -ísimo  |
| amable    | amabil**ísimo** | drop -e, add -ísimo  |

**Examples:**

5. **La paella estaba buenísima** (The paella was extremely good)
6. **El verano en Valencia es calurosísimo** (Summer in Valencia is extremely hot)
7. **Las Fallas son popularísimas** (Las Fallas are extremely popular)
8. **La horchata está riquísima** (The horchata is extremely delicious)

## 💬 Common Expressions with Comparatives

| Expression           | Meaning             | Example                                  |
| -------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| cada vez más         | more and more       | Valencia me gusta **cada vez más**       |
| cada vez menos       | less and less       | Llueve **cada vez menos** en verano      |
| cuanto más...más     | the more...the more | **Cuanto más** estudio, **más** aprendo  |
| cuanto menos...menos | the less...the less | **Cuanto menos** duermo, **menos** rindo |

1. **Cada vez más turistas visitan Valencia** (More and more tourists visit Valencia)
2. **Cuanto más practico español, más fácil es** (The more I practice Spanish, the easier it is)
3. **Me siento cada vez más cómodo aquí** (I feel more and more comfortable here)

## 🍊 Valencia Comparisons

**Comparing barrios:**

1. **Ruzafa es más moderno que El Carmen** (Ruzafa is more modern than El Carmen)
2. **El Cabanyal es tan interesante como Benimaclet** (El Cabanyal is as interesting as Benimaclet)
3. **El centro es el barrio más turístico de Valencia** (Downtown is the most touristy neighborhood in Valencia)

**Comparing cities:**

4. **Valencia es más tranquila que Barcelona** (Valencia is calmer than Barcelona)
5. **Valencia tiene mejor clima que Madrid** (Valencia has better weather than Madrid)
6. **Es la tercera ciudad más grande de España** (It's the third biggest city in Spain)

**Comparing food:**

7. **La paella valenciana es mejor que la de otros sitios** (Valencian paella is better than elsewhere)
8. **La horchata es tan típica como la paella** (Horchata is as typical as paella)
9. **¡Esta fideuà está buenísima!** (This fideuà is extremely good!)

## ✏️ Practice Exercises

### A. Form Comparatives

Complete using más...que, menos...que, tan...como, or tanto...como:

1. El verano es \_\_\_ caluroso \_\_\_ el invierno (more...than)
2. Mi hija es \_\_\_ alta \_\_\_ su amiga (as...as)
3. No tengo \_\_\_ tiempo \_\_\_ antes (as much...as)
4. El autobús es \_\_\_ cómodo \_\_\_ el metro (less...than)
5. Valencia tiene \_\_\_ playas \_\_\_ Alicante (as many...as)

### B. Translate Superlatives

Translate into Spanish:

1. It's the best restaurant in Valencia
2. She's the tallest girl in her class
3. The paella was extremely delicious
4. It's the least expensive option
5. The more I study, the more I learn

### C. Write Comparisons About Valencia

Complete with your own ideas:

1. Valencia es más \_\_\_ que \_\_\_
2. El mejor \_\_\_ de Valencia es \_\_\_
3. Valencia tiene tantos \_\_\_ como \_\_\_
4. Me gusta Valencia cada vez \_\_\_
5. La playa es \_\_\_ísima en verano
