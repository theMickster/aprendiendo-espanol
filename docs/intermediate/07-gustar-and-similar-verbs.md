# 🔄 Gustar and Similar Verbs

## It Doesn't Work Like English -- And That's OK!

**⏱️ Study Time: 10-15 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Master the "backwards" verbs that express likes, dislikes, and feelings!**

---

## Overview

In English we say "I like coffee." In Spanish, the structure flips: **Me gusta el café** -- literally "Coffee is pleasing to me." The thing you like is the SUBJECT, and you are the indirect object. Once you get this pattern, a whole family of verbs opens up.

---

## 1️⃣ How Gustar Actually Works

**English:** I like the book.
**Spanish:** Me gusta el libro. (The book is pleasing to me.)

The verb agrees with the THING liked, not the person:

- **Me gusta el libro** (I like the book -- singular thing)
- **Me gustan los libros** (I like the books -- plural things)

---

## 2️⃣ The Full Pattern

| Emphasis (optional)           | Indirect Object Pronoun | gusta / gustan | Example                          |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------- |
| A mí                          | me                      | gusta / gustan | A mí me gusta el café            |
| A ti                          | te                      | gusta / gustan | A ti te gustan los perros        |
| A él / a ella / a usted       | le                      | gusta / gustan | A ella le gusta bailar           |
| A nosotros/as                 | nos                     | gusta / gustan | A nosotros nos gustan las playas |
| A vosotros/as                 | os                      | gusta / gustan | A vosotros os gusta Valencia     |
| A ellos / a ellas / a ustedes | les                     | gusta / gustan | A ellos les gustan las fallas    |

**The "a mí," "a ti," etc. part is optional** -- use it for emphasis or to clarify who "le" or "les" refers to:

1. **Me gusta el arroz** (I like rice)
2. **A mí me gusta el arroz, pero a ella no le gusta** (I like rice, but she doesn't)
3. **¿Te gustan las paellas?** (Do you like paellas?)
4. **A mis hijas les gusta la playa** (My daughters like the beach)

---

## 3️⃣ Gustar with Infinitives

When what you like is an ACTION, use **gusta** (always singular -- no gustan):

5. **Me gusta bailar** (I like to dance)
6. **Nos gusta pasear por el Turia** (We like walking along the Turia)
7. **¿Te gusta cocinar?** (Do you like to cook?)
8. **A los niños les gusta jugar en el parque** (The kids like playing in the park)

---

## 4️⃣ The Gustar Family -- Similar Verbs

All of these work exactly like gustar:

| Verb          | Meaning                        | Example                                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **encantar**  | to love (stronger than gustar) | Me encanta Valencia (I love Valencia)                    |
| **molestar**  | to bother                      | Me molesta el ruido (The noise bothers me)               |
| **interesar** | to interest                    | Me interesan las fallas (The Fallas interest me)         |
| **importar**  | to matter                      | No me importa el precio (The price doesn't matter to me) |
| **faltar**    | to lack / to need              | Me falta práctica (I lack practice)                      |
| **quedar**    | to have left / to remain       | Me quedan dos euros (I have two euros left)              |
| **doler**     | to hurt                        | Me duele la cabeza (My head hurts)                       |
| **parecer**   | to seem                        | Me parece bien (It seems fine to me)                     |
| **apetecer**  | to feel like (very Spanish!)   | ¿Te apetece un café? (Do you feel like a coffee?)        |

### Examples in context:

9. **Me encantan las playas de Valencia** (I love Valencia's beaches)
10. **¿Os molesta si abro la ventana?** (Does it bother you if I open the window?)
11. **A mi hija le interesan los dinosaurios** (My daughter is interested in dinosaurs)
12. **No nos importa esperar** (We don't mind waiting)
13. **Me faltan dos ingredientes para la paella** (I'm missing two ingredients for the paella)
14. **¿Cuánto dinero te queda?** (How much money do you have left?)
15. **Me duelen los pies después de caminar** (My feet hurt after walking)
16. **¿Qué te parece el restaurante?** (What do you think of the restaurant?)
17. **¿Te apetece ir a la playa?** (Do you feel like going to the beach?)

---

## 5️⃣ Gustar in Past Tense

### Imperfect (habitual/ongoing likes):

18. **De niño me gustaba el chocolate** (As a kid I liked chocolate)
19. **Nos encantaba ir a la playa** (We loved going to the beach)
20. **Me dolía la espalda cada mañana** (My back hurt every morning)

### Preterite (specific occasion):

21. **Me gustó mucho la película** (I really liked the movie)
22. **¿Te gustó la paella?** (Did you like the paella?)
23. **Nos encantó el concierto** (We loved the concert)
24. **Me dolió mucho la caída** (The fall hurt a lot)

**Rule of thumb:** gustaba = I used to like / I liked (in general). gustó = I liked (that specific thing).

---

## 🍊 Cultural Note

**Apetecer** is very common in Spain but rarely used in Latin America. You'll hear it constantly in Valencia:

- **¿Te apetece una horchata?** (Do you feel like a horchata?)
- **No me apetece salir hoy** (I don't feel like going out today)
- **¿Os apetece cenar fuera?** (Do you all feel like eating out?)

It's one of those words that instantly makes your Spanish sound more "from Spain."

## ✏️ Practice Exercises

### A) Complete with the correct form (gusta/gustan/encanta/encantan, etc.):

1. A mí me \_\_\_ el café con leche (gustar)
2. A mis hijas les \_\_\_ los helados (encantar)
3. ¿Te \_\_\_ los museos? (interesar)
4. Me \_\_\_ las piernas después de correr (doler)
5. Nos \_\_\_ cinco euros para el autobús (faltar)
6. ¿Os \_\_\_ ir al cine esta noche? (apetecer)

### B) Translate using gustar-type verbs:

7. I love the beaches in Valencia → \_\_\_\_\_\_
8. My head hurts → \_\_\_\_\_\_
9. We don't mind the rain → \_\_\_\_\_\_
10. Do you (tú) feel like a coffee? → \_\_\_\_\_\_
11. The noise bothers my daughters → \_\_\_\_\_\_
12. I liked the movie (specific) → \_\_\_\_\_\_

### C) Conversation practice -- answer these questions:

13. ¿Qué te gusta hacer los fines de semana?
14. ¿Te gustaba España antes de mudarte?
15. ¿Qué te parece la comida valenciana?
