# 🔁 Present Continuous (Estar + Gerund)

## Describe What's Happening Right Now!

**⏱️ Study Time: 10-15 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Use estar + gerund to talk about actions happening NOW and in the past!**

---

## Overview

The present continuous (also called the present progressive) is one of the most-used structures in daily Spanish conversation. It tells people what is happening **right now**, at this very moment. The formula is simple: a form of ESTAR + a gerund (-ando/-iendo). You already know ESTAR from [beginner/03](../beginner/03-building-block-verbs.md) and its past forms from [intermediate/01](01-building-block-verbs-past-tense.md). Now you just need to learn how to form the gerund.

---

## 1️⃣ Forming the Gerund

The gerund is the Spanish equivalent of English "-ing." Drop the infinitive ending and add:

| Verb Type | Rule              | Example               |
| --------- | ----------------- | --------------------- |
| **-AR**   | stem + **-ando**  | hablar → habl**ando** |
| **-ER**   | stem + **-iendo** | comer → com**iendo**  |
| **-IR**   | stem + **-iendo** | vivir → viv**iendo**  |

**More examples:**

| Infinitive | Gerund      | English  |
| ---------- | ----------- | -------- |
| caminar    | caminando   | walking  |
| estudiar   | estudiando  | studying |
| cocinar    | cocinando   | cooking  |
| correr     | corriendo   | running  |
| aprender   | aprendiendo | learning |
| escribir   | escribiendo | writing  |
| abrir      | abriendo    | opening  |

---

## 2️⃣ Irregular Gerunds

Some verbs change their stem or spelling in the gerund. These are the most common ones:

| Infinitive | Gerund        | Why                               |
| ---------- | ------------- | --------------------------------- |
| leer       | **leyendo**   | Unstressed -i- between vowels → y |
| dormir     | **durmiendo** | o → u stem change                 |
| decir      | **diciendo**  | e → i stem change                 |
| pedir      | **pidiendo**  | e → i stem change                 |
| ir         | **yendo**     | Fully irregular                   |
| poder      | **pudiendo**  | o → u stem change                 |
| venir      | **viniendo**  | e → i stem change                 |
| sentir     | **sintiendo** | e → i stem change                 |

**💡 Pattern:** -IR verbs with e→i or o→u stem changes in the present tense also change in the gerund. The -ER/-IR "unstressed i between vowels" becomes **y** (leer → leyendo, traer → trayendo, oír → oyendo).

---

## 3️⃣ Present Continuous: ESTAR (present) + Gerund

Combine the present tense of ESTAR with any gerund:

**Full conjugation with HABLAR:**

| Person              | ESTAR   | + Gerund | English                   |
| ------------------- | ------- | -------- | ------------------------- |
| Yo                  | estoy   | hablando | I am speaking             |
| Tú                  | estás   | hablando | you are speaking          |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | está    | hablando | he/she is speaking        |
| Nosotros/as         | estamos | hablando | we are speaking           |
| Vosotros/as         | estáis  | hablando | you all are speaking      |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | están   | hablando | they/you all are speaking |

**That's it!** ESTAR changes for the person, the gerund stays the same every time.

---

## 4️⃣ When to Use Present Continuous vs Simple Present

This is a common source of confusion. Here is the key difference:

| Present Continuous             | Simple Present              |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Happening **right now**        | Habitual or general truth   |
| Temporary situation            | Routine or regular activity |
| "I am eating" (at this moment) | "I eat paella" (in general) |

**Examples:**

1. **Estoy comiendo paella** → I am eating paella (right now, at this moment)
   **Como paella los domingos** → I eat paella on Sundays (habitual)

2. **Estoy estudiando español** → I am studying Spanish (right now)
   **Estudio español cada día** → I study Spanish every day (routine)

3. **Está lloviendo** → It is raining (right now)
   **Llueve mucho en otoño** → It rains a lot in autumn (general)

**⚠️ Note:** Spanish uses the present continuous **less** than English. In English you might say "I'm living in Valencia" for a temporary situation, but in Spanish you would normally say "Vivo en Valencia" (simple present), even if it is temporary.

## 5️⃣ Past Continuous: ESTABA + Gerund

Replace the present ESTAR with the imperfect **ESTABA** to describe what **was happening** in the past:

| Person              | ESTAR (imperfect) | + Gerund | English                |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | -------- | ---------------------- |
| Yo                  | estaba            | hablando | I was speaking         |
| Tú                  | estabas           | hablando | you were speaking      |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | estaba            | hablando | he/she was speaking    |
| Nosotros/as         | estábamos         | hablando | we were speaking       |
| Vosotros/as         | estabais          | hablando | you all were speaking  |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | estaban           | hablando | they/you were speaking |

This connects directly to the **interruption pattern** from [Lesson 04](04-preterite-vs-imperfect-beyond-basics.md):

**Estaba + gerund + cuando + preterite**

- **Estaba cocinando cuando sonó el timbre** (I was cooking when the doorbell rang)
- **Estábamos durmiendo cuando empezó la tormenta** (We were sleeping when the storm started)

## 6️⃣ Valencia Life Examples

Describe your daily life in Valencia right now:

1. **Estoy aprendiendo español en Valencia**

   - (I am learning Spanish in Valencia)

2. **Los niños están jugando en la playa de la Malvarrosa**

   - (The kids are playing at Malvarrosa beach)

3. **Mamá está comprando naranjas en el Mercado Central**

   - (Mom is buying oranges at the Central Market)

4. **Papá está corriendo por el Jardín del Turia**

   - (Dad is running through the Turia Garden)

5. **Estamos comiendo horchata y fartons en una horchatería**

   - (We are eating horchata and fartons at a horchata shop)

6. **Las niñas están escribiendo postales a los abuelos**

   - (The girls are writing postcards to their grandparents)

7. **Está lloviendo, así que estamos leyendo en casa**

   - (It is raining, so we are reading at home)

8. **¿Qué estás haciendo?** - **Estoy estudiando para el examen**
   - (What are you doing? - I am studying for the exam)

## 🎯 Quick Reference Card

| What you want to say      | Formula                              | Example                         |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Happening right now       | ESTAR (present) + gerund             | Estoy comiendo                  |
| Was happening in the past | ESTAR (imperfect) + gerund           | Estaba comiendo                 |
| -AR gerund                | stem + -ando                         | hablar → hablando               |
| -ER/-IR gerund            | stem + -iendo                        | comer → comiendo                |
| Interrupted past action   | Estaba + gerund + cuando + preterite | Estaba comiendo cuando llegaste |

## 💡 Cultural Note: Right Now in Valencia

You will hear the present continuous constantly in Valencia. At the mercado: "¿Qué estás buscando?" (What are you looking for?). At school pickup: "¿Qué estaban haciendo hoy?" (What were they doing today?). On the phone: "Estoy llegando" (I'm arriving -- even if you just left the house!). Spaniards love using "estoy llegando" even when they are still far away. It is a cultural classic!
