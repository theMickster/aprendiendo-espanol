# 🧱 Building Block Verbs - Future Tense

## Talk About Tomorrow: Future Tense with the 6 Essential Verbs!

**⏱️ Study Time: 15-20 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Use the 6 building block verbs to talk about the FUTURE!**

---

## Two Ways to Talk About the Future

Good news! Spanish has TWO ways to talk about the future, and one is super easy!

### 🎯 IR + A + Infinitive (Going to...)

**The EASY way - you already know this!**

- Uses present tense IR + A + any verb
- "I'm going to study" = Voy a estudiar
- Most common in everyday conversation
- More immediate/planned future

**Example:** Voy a comer (I'm going to eat)

---

### 🔮 Simple Future (Will...)

**The FORMAL way - new verb endings!**

- Add endings to the infinitive
- "I will study" = Estudiaré
- Sounds more formal/distant
- Expresses promises, predictions, uncertainty

**Example:** Comeré (I will eat)

---

## Quick Comparison

| Situation          | IR + A + Infinitive | Simple Future  |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | -------------- |
| "I'm going to eat" | Voy a comer         | Comeré         |
| Planning           | ✅ Very common      | Less common    |
| Predictions        | OK                  | ✅ More common |
| Promises           | OK                  | ✅ More common |
| Casual speech      | ✅ Natural          | More formal    |

**💡 For A1-A2:** Focus on IR + A first! It's easier and more common in daily conversation.

## 1️⃣ SER - To Be (Future)

### 🎯 IR + A + SER (Going to be)

**Formula:** IR (conjugated) + A + SER

| Person   | Spanish     | English               |
| -------- | ----------- | --------------------- |
| Yo       | voy a ser   | I'm going to be       |
| Tú       | vas a ser   | you're going to be    |
| Él/Ella  | va a ser    | he/she is going to be |
| Nosotros | vamos a ser | we're going to be     |
| Ellos    | van a ser   | they're going to be   |

**Examples:**

1. **Voy a ser profesor**

   - (I'm going to be a teacher)

2. **Va a ser un día bonito**

   - (It's going to be a nice day)

3. **Vamos a ser amigos**
   - (We're going to be friends)

---

### 🔮 Simple Future: SERÉ

**Add these endings to SER stem: SER-**

| Person   | Spanish | English        |
| -------- | ------- | -------------- |
| Yo       | seré    | I will be      |
| Tú       | serás   | you will be    |
| Él/Ella  | será    | he/she will be |
| Nosotros | seremos | we will be     |
| Ellos    | serán   | they will be   |

**Examples:**

1. **Seré doctor algún día**

   - (I will be a doctor someday)

2. **Será difícil**

   - (It will be difficult)

3. **Seremos felices**
   - (We will be happy)

## 🎯 When to Use Which Future?

### Use IR + A + Infinitive for:

✅ **Immediate plans** (today, tomorrow, this week)

- Voy a comer en una hora (I'm going to eat in an hour)

✅ **Decided plans** (things you've already planned)

- Vamos a ir de vacaciones (We're going to go on vacation)

✅ **Casual conversation** (everyday speech)

- ¿Qué vas a hacer? (What are you going to do?)

✅ **Things about to happen**

- Va a llover (It's going to rain - I see clouds!)

---

### Use Simple Future for:

✅ **Distant future** (someday, in the future)

- Algún día seré rico (Someday I will be rich)

✅ **Predictions** (guessing about the future)

- Será difícil (It will be difficult)

✅ **Promises** (committing to do something)

- Te llamaré (I will call you)

✅ **Uncertainty/wondering** (making guesses)

- ¿Dónde estará? (Where could he be?)

✅ **Formal writing/speech**

- Los resultados estarán listos (The results will be ready)

## 💪 Real-Life Scenarios

### 🌅 Tomorrow's Plans (IR + A)

"Mañana **voy a ir** a la universidad. **Voy a tener** tres clases. Por la tarde **voy a hacer** ejercicio. Después **voy a estar** en casa. **Va a hacer** buen tiempo. **Va a haber** mucho que hacer."

_Translation: Tomorrow I'm going to go to the university. I'm going to have three classes. In the afternoon I'm going to exercise. Later I'm going to be at home. The weather is going to be nice. There's going to be a lot to do._

---

### 🔮 Life Predictions (Simple Future)

"Algún día **seré** profesor. **Tendré** mi propia escuela. **Haré** lo que me gusta. **Estaré** feliz. **Será** una buena vida. **Habrá** muchos desafíos pero **iré** adelante."

_Translation: Someday I will be a teacher. I will have my own school. I will do what I like. I will be happy. It will be a good life. There will be many challenges but I will go forward._

---

### 🎯 This Weekend (IR + A)

"Este fin de semana **vamos a ir** a la playa. **Va a hacer** sol. **Voy a estar** con mi familia. **Vamos a hacer** un picnic. **Va a ser** divertido. **Va a haber** mucha gente pero no importa."

_Translation: This weekend we're going to go to the beach. It's going to be sunny. I'm going to be with my family. We're going to have a picnic. It's going to be fun. There's going to be many people but it doesn't matter._

---

### 📅 Next Year (Mixed)

"El año próximo **iré** a la universidad. **Estudiaré** medicina. **Será** difícil pero **voy a estudiar** mucho. **Voy a hacer** nuevos amigos. **Tendré** que trabajar duro. **Habrá** muchos exámenes. Pero **seré** doctor algún día."

_Translation: Next year I will go to university. I will study medicine. It will be difficult but I'm going to study a lot. I'm going to make new friends. I will have to work hard. There will be many exams. But I will be a doctor someday._

### Simple Future Endings

**Regular verbs:** Add to infinitive

- Yo: -é
- Tú: -ás
- Él/Ella: -á
- Nosotros: -emos
- Ellos: -án

**Examples:**

- hablar → hablaré, hablarás, hablará...
- comer → comeré, comerás, comerá...
- vivir → viviré, vivirás, vivirá...

**Irregular stems (memorize these!):**

- TENER → TENDR-
- HACER → HAR-
- HABER → HABR-
- poder → podr-
- saber → sabr-
- poner → pondr-
- salir → saldr-
- venir → vendr-
- querer → querr-
- decir → dir-

## Common Time Expressions

### With IR + A (Near Future)

- mañana (tomorrow)
- esta noche (tonight)
- este fin de semana (this weekend)
- la próxima semana (next week)
- el próximo mes (next month)
- pronto (soon)
- más tarde (later)
- en una hora (in an hour)

### With Simple Future (Distant/Uncertain)

- algún día (someday)
- en el futuro (in the future)
- el año que viene (next year)
- dentro de... (within...)
- cuando sea mayor (when I'm older)
- probablemente (probably)
- tal vez (perhaps)
- quizás (maybe)

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with IR + A** - It's easier and more common!

   - "Voy a estudiar" feels more natural than "Estudiaré"

2. **Simple future = formal** - Use it for:

   - Promises: "Te llamaré" (I will call you)
   - Predictions: "Será difícil" (It will be difficult)
   - Formal writing: "Los resultados estarán listos" (Results will be ready)

3. **Practice daily** - Every morning, say:

   - "Hoy voy a..." (Today I'm going to...)
   - "Mañana voy a..." (Tomorrow I'm going to...)
   - "Algún día seré..." (Someday I will be...)

4. **Remember the irregulars** - Only 3 in your power verbs:

   - TENER → TENDRÉ
   - HACER → HARÉ
   - HABER → HABRÁ

5. **Context is key**:
   - Near future = IR + A
   - Distant/uncertain = Simple future
   - When in doubt, use IR + A!
