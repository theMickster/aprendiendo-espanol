# 📋 Tener que, Hay que, Deber -- Expressing Obligation

## Three Ways to Say "You Have To" (And When to Use Each)

**⏱️ Study Time: 10-15 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Choose the right obligation expression for every situation!**

---

## Overview

Spanish has three main ways to say someone "has to" or "must" do something. Each carries a different weight and meaning:

- **Tener que** = personal obligation ("I have to...")
- **Hay que** = impersonal / general rule ("One must..." / "You have to...")
- **Deber** = moral duty or recommendation ("You should...")

---

## 1️⃣ Tener que + Infinitive -- Personal Obligation

This is the most common way to express obligation. It's personal -- tied to a specific person.

### Present Tense

| Person              | Conjugation | Example                                  |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Yo                  | tengo que   | Tengo que estudiar (I have to study)     |
| Tú                  | tienes que  | Tienes que comer (You have to eat)       |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | tiene que   | Tiene que trabajar (He/she has to work)  |
| Nosotros/as         | tenemos que | Tenemos que ir (We have to go)           |
| Vosotros/as         | tenéis que  | Tenéis que salir (You all have to leave) |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | tienen que  | Tienen que venir (They have to come)     |

1. **Tengo que ir al supermercado** (I have to go to the supermarket)
2. **Las niñas tienen que hacer los deberes** (The girls have to do homework)
3. **Tenemos que renovar el NIE** (We have to renew the NIE)
4. **¿Tienes que trabajar mañana?** (Do you have to work tomorrow?)

---

## 2️⃣ Hay que + Infinitive -- Impersonal Obligation

No conjugation -- **hay que** is always the same. It expresses what "one must" do in general, without pointing to a specific person.

5. **Hay que estudiar para aprender** (You have to study to learn)
6. **Hay que reservar mesa en ese restaurante** (You have to book a table at that restaurant)
7. **Hay que tener paciencia con los niños** (You have to have patience with kids)
8. **Hay que llevar protección solar en la playa** (You have to wear sunscreen at the beach)

**When to use it:** Rules, general advice, signs, instructions -- whenever it applies to everyone, not just one person.

---

## 3️⃣ Deber + Infinitive -- Moral Obligation / Should

Deber expresses what you SHOULD do -- a recommendation or moral duty, softer than "tener que."

### Present Tense

| Person              | Conjugation | Example                                               |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Yo                  | debo        | Debo estudiar más (I should study more)               |
| Tú                  | debes       | Debes descansar (You should rest)                     |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | debe        | Debe ser más puntual (He/she should be more punctual) |
| Nosotros/as         | debemos     | Debemos practicar (We should practice)                |
| Vosotros/as         | debéis      | Debéis escuchar (You all should listen)               |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | deben       | Deben llegar a tiempo (They should arrive on time)    |

9. **Debes comer más fruta** (You should eat more fruit)
10. **Debemos hablar más español en casa** (We should speak more Spanish at home)
11. **No debes preocuparte tanto** (You shouldn't worry so much)

### Deber de + Infinitive = Probability (Different Meaning!)

**⚠️ Important:** Adding **de** changes the meaning completely:

12. **Debe de estar en casa** (He must be at home -- probably is)
13. **Deben de ser las tres** (It must be three o'clock -- I think)
14. **Debe de costar mucho** (It must cost a lot -- I imagine)

**Deber + inf** = obligation (should). **Deber de + inf** = probability (must be).

---

## 4️⃣ Strength Scale

From weakest to strongest obligation:

| Expression    | Strength           | Translation       | Feeling                  |
| ------------- | ------------------ | ----------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Deber**     | Recommendation     | should            | "It would be good if..." |
| **Tener que** | Personal necessity | have to           | "I need to do this"      |
| **Hay que**   | Universal rule     | must / one has to | "Everyone must"          |

15. **Debes estudiar más** (You should study more -- friendly advice)
16. **Tienes que estudiar más** (You have to study more -- real need)
17. **Hay que estudiar para aprobar** (You must study to pass -- general truth)

---

## 5️⃣ Past Tense Forms

### Tener que in the past:

| Tense     | Form (yo) | Example                                               |
| --------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Imperfect | tenía que | Tenía que trabajar cada día (I had to work every day) |
| Preterite | tuve que  | Tuve que ir al hospital (I had to go to the hospital) |

18. **Tenía que madrugar cuando vivía en Olympia** (I had to get up early when I lived in Olympia)
19. **Tuve que llamar al fontanero ayer** (I had to call the plumber yesterday)

### Hay que in the past:

| Tense     | Form      | Example                                                  |
| --------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Imperfect | había que | Había que esperar mucho (You had to wait a lot)          |
| Preterite | hubo que  | Hubo que cancelar la fiesta (We had to cancel the party) |

20. **Antes había que hacer cola para todo** (Before, you had to queue for everything)
21. **Hubo que evacuar el edificio** (They had to evacuate the building)

### Deber in the past:

| Tense     | Form (yo) | Example                                              |
| --------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Imperfect | debía     | Debía estudiar más (I should have studied more)      |
| Preterite | debí      | No debí comer tanto (I shouldn't have eaten so much) |

---

## 6️⃣ Bonus: Poder for Ability/Permission

While not obligation, **poder** often pairs with these expressions:

22. **¿Puedo sentarme aquí?** (Can I sit here? -- permission)
23. **No puedo ir, tengo que trabajar** (I can't go, I have to work)
24. **No se puede fumar aquí** (You can't smoke here -- it's not allowed)
25. **Se puede aparcar en esa calle** (You can park on that street -- it's permitted)

---

## 🏖️ Valencia Daily Life Examples

### At the beach:

26. **Hay que ducharse antes de entrar en la piscina** (You have to shower before entering the pool)
27. **Tienes que ponerte crema solar** (You have to put on sunscreen)
28. **No se puede bañar cuando la bandera es roja** (You can't swim when the flag is red)

### For the empadronamiento:

29. **Hay que pedir cita previa** (You have to request an appointment)
30. **Tenemos que llevar el contrato de alquiler** (We have to bring the rental contract)
31. **Debemos llegar temprano** (We should arrive early)

### School obligations:

32. **Las niñas tienen que hacer los deberes antes de jugar** (The girls have to do homework before playing)
33. **Hay que llevar uniforme al colegio** (You have to wear a uniform to school)
34. **Deben estudiar para el examen** (They should study for the exam)
