## Two Past Tenses in Spanish

Spanish has TWO main past tenses. Don't worry - you'll get the hang of it!

### 🔄 Imperfect (Imperfecto)

**Use for:**

- How things USED TO BE
- What you DID REGULARLY
- Ongoing descriptions in the past
- "I was...", "I used to...", "I would..."

**Keywords:** cuando era niño, siempre, cada día, todos los días

### ✅ Preterite (Pretérito)

**Use for:**

- What you DID on a specific occasion
- Completed actions with clear beginning/end
- "I did...", "I went...", "It was..." (specific time)

**Keywords:** ayer, la semana pasada, en 2020, el martes

## 1️⃣ SER - To Be (Past)

### 🔄 ERA (Imperfect) - How things WERE

| Person              | Spanish | English              |
| ------------------- | ------- | -------------------- |
| Yo                  | era     | I was / used to be   |
| Tú                  | eras    | you were             |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | era     | he/she was, you were |
| Nosotros/as         | éramos  | we were              |
| Vosotros/as         | erais   | you all were         |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | eran    | they/you all were    |

### ✅ FUI (Preterite) - What you WERE on a specific occasion

| Person              | Spanish  | English              |
| ------------------- | -------- | -------------------- |
| Yo                  | fui      | I was                |
| Tú                  | fuiste   | you were             |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | fue      | he/she was, you were |
| Nosotros/as         | fuimos   | we were              |
| Vosotros/as         | fuisteis | you all were         |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | fueron   | they/you all were    |

**⚠️ Note:** FUI/FUE is the SAME for SER and IR! Context tells you which!

---

### 📖 Using SER in Past

**With ERA (ongoing/habitual):**

1. **Cuando era niño, era muy tímido**

   - (When I was a child, I was very shy)

2. **Mi escuela era grande**

   - (My school was big)

3. **Antes éramos vecinos**

   - (We used to be neighbors)

4. **El clima era diferente**
   - (The climate was different)

**With FUI (specific occasion):**

5. **Ayer fue un día difícil**

   - (Yesterday was a difficult day)

6. **La fiesta fue increíble**

   - (The party was incredible)

7. **Fui estudiante durante cuatro años**

   - (I was a student for four years)

8. **El examen fue fácil**
   - (The exam was easy)

---

### ✏️ Practice: ERA or FUI?

4. La película **\_\_\_** aburrida anoche (was - specific)
5. Antes **\_\_\_** compañeros de clase (we were - used to be)

## 2️⃣ ESTAR - To Be (Past)

### 🔄 ESTABA (Imperfect) - How you FELT habitually

| Person              | Spanish   | English              |
| ------------------- | --------- | -------------------- |
| Yo                  | estaba    | I was                |
| Tú                  | estabas   | you were             |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | estaba    | he/she was, you were |
| Nosotros/as         | estábamos | we were              |
| Vosotros/as         | estabais  | you all were         |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | estaban   | they/you all were    |

### ✅ ESTUVE (Preterite) - How you FELT at a specific time

| Person              | Spanish     | English              |
| ------------------- | ----------- | -------------------- |
| Yo                  | estuve      | I was                |
| Tú                  | estuviste   | you were             |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | estuvo      | he/she was, you were |
| Nosotros/as         | estuvimos   | we were              |
| Vosotros/as         | estuvisteis | you all were         |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | estuvieron  | they/you all were    |

---

### 📖 Using ESTAR in Past

**With ESTABA (ongoing feelings/states):**

1. **Siempre estaba cansado en la universidad**

   - (I was always tired in university)

2. **La puerta estaba abierta**

   - (The door was open)

3. **Estábamos contentos en aquella ciudad**

   - (We were happy in that city)

4. **Cuando llegué, él estaba en casa**
   - (When I arrived, he was at home)

**With ESTUVE (specific moments):**

5. **Ayer estuve en la biblioteca**

   - (Yesterday I was in the library)

6. **Estuve enfermo la semana pasada**

   - (I was sick last week)

7. **Estuvimos en Madrid tres días**

   - (We were in Madrid for three days)

8. **El tiempo estuvo perfecto el domingo**
   - (The weather was perfect on Sunday)

---

| Person              | Spanish | English               |
| ------------------- | ------- | --------------------- |
| Yo                  | iba     | I went / used to go   |
| Tú                  | ibas    | you went              |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | iba     | he/she went, you went |
| Nosotros/as         | íbamos  | we went               |
| Vosotros/as         | ibais   | you all went          |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | iban    | they/you all went     |

### ✅ FUI (Preterite) - Where you WENT on a specific occasion

| Person              | Spanish  | English               |
| ------------------- | -------- | --------------------- |
| Yo                  | fui      | I went                |
| Tú                  | fuiste   | you went              |
| Él/Ella/Usted       | fue      | he/she went, you went |

| Vosotros/as         | fuisteis | you all went          |
| Ellos/Ellas/Ustedes | fueron   | they/you all went     |

**⚠️ Remember:** FUI is the same as SER preterite!

---

### 📖 Using IR in Past

**With IBA (habitual):**

1. **Cuando era niño, iba a la escuela en autobús**

   - (When I was a child, I went to school by bus)

2. **Íbamos al parque cada domingo**

   - (We went to the park every Sunday)

3. **Siempre iba al gimnasio por la mañana**

   - (I always went to the gym in the morning)

4. **Mi abuela iba a la iglesia los domingos**
   - (My grandmother went to church on Sundays)

**With FUI (specific trip):**

5. **Ayer fui al supermercado**

   - (Yesterday I went to the supermarket)

6. **Fuimos a la playa el fin de semana pasado**

   - (We went to the beach last weekend)

7. **¿Adónde fuiste de vacaciones?**

   - (Where did you go on vacation?)

8. **Fueron al cine anoche**
   - (They went to the movies last night)

---

## 6️⃣ HABER - There was/were (Past)

### 🔄 HABÍA (Imperfect) - There WAS/WERE habitually

**Only one form: HABÍA**
(For both singular and plural)

### ✅ HUBO (Preterite) - There WAS/WERE on a specific occasion

**Only one form: HUBO**
(For both singular and plural)

---

### 📖 Using HABER in Past

**With HABÍA (how things used to be):**

1. **Antes había muchos árboles aquí**

   - (Before there were many trees here)

2. **En mi ciudad había un cine antiguo**

   - (In my city there was an old cinema)

3. **Siempre había mucha gente en el parque**

   - (There were always many people in the park)

4. **No había internet cuando era niño**
   - (There was no internet when I was a child)

**With HUBO (specific event):**

5. **Ayer hubo un accidente en la calle**

   - (Yesterday there was an accident on the street)

6. **Hubo una fiesta el sábado**

   - (There was a party on Saturday)

7. **Hubo muchos problemas en la reunión**
   - (There were many problems in the meeting)

**As auxiliary (present perfect - uses HE/HAS/HA/etc):**

8. **He visitado París**

   - (I have visited Paris)

9. **Has estudiado mucho**

   - (You have studied a lot)

10. **Hemos vivido aquí dos años**
    - (We have lived here two years)

---

3. Cuando era niño, **\_\_\_** menos coches (there were)
4. El lunes **\_\_\_** una reunión (there was)
5. Siempre **\_\_\_** comida en la nevera (there was)

## 🎯 Quick Decision Guide

### When to use IMPERFECT vs PRETERITE

| Imperfect            | Preterite                  |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Habitual actions     | One-time completed actions |
| "Used to..."         | "I did..."                 |
| Ongoing descriptions | Specific completed events  |
| No specific time     | Specific time mentioned    |
| Background info      | Main events                |

**Keywords:**

**IMPERFECT:**

- siempre (always)
- cada día (every day)
- todos los días (every day)
- generalmente (generally)
- cuando era niño (when I was a child)

**PRETERITE:**

- ayer (yesterday)
- la semana pasada (last week)
- el año pasado (last year)
- en 2020 (in 2020)
- el martes (on Tuesday)
- una vez (once)

## 💪 Combining Verbs in Past Tense

### Real Stories Using Both Tenses

**Story 1: Childhood**

"Cuando **era** niño, **iba** a la escuela cada día. **Tenía** muchos amigos y **hacíamos** deporte juntos. Siempre **había** mucho que hacer. **Estaba** feliz en aquellos días."

_Translation: When I was a child, I went to school every day. I had many friends and we did sports together. There was always a lot to do. I was happy in those days._

---

**Story 2: Last Weekend**

"El fin de semana pasado **fue** especial. El sábado **hice** ejercicio por la mañana. **Hacía** buen tiempo. Después **fui** al supermercado porque **tuve** que hacer la compra. **Estuve** ocupado pero contento. Por la tarde **hubo** una fiesta."

_Translation: Last weekend was special. On Saturday I exercised in the morning. The weather was nice. Then I went to the supermarket because I had to do the shopping. I was busy but happy. In the afternoon there was a party._

---

**Story 3: Mixed - Then and Now**

"Antes **vivía** en otra ciudad. **Era** más pequeña y **había** menos gente. Cada día **iba** al trabajo en autobús. **Tenía** más tiempo libre entonces. Un día **decidí** mudarme. **Fue** difícil al principio, pero ahora estoy contento aquí."

_Translation: Before I lived in another city. It was smaller and there were fewer people. Every day I went to work by bus. I had more free time then. One day I decided to move. It was difficult at first, but now I'm happy here._
