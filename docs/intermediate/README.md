# 🌿 Intermediate Lessons (B1-B2)

Ready to take your Spanish to the next level!

## 🎯 Level Description

**B1 Level (Threshold):** Handle most everyday situations, express opinions and plans  
**B2 Level (Vantage):** Interact with native speakers naturally, understand complex texts

You're in the right place if:

- You can hold basic conversations
- You know present tense well
- You're ready for past and future tenses
- You want to express more complex ideas

## 📚 Suggested Learning Path

### Grammar Expansion

1. **Preterite Tense** - Talk about completed actions
2. **Imperfect Tense** - Describe ongoing past actions
3. **Preterite vs Imperfect** - Choose the right past tense
4. **Future Tense** - Talk about what will happen
5. **Conditional Tense** - Express hypothetical situations
6. **Present Perfect** - Discuss recent events
7. **Introduction to Subjunctive** - Express wishes and doubts

### Advanced Structures

8. **Object Pronouns** - Me, te, lo, la, les...
9. **Reflexive Verbs** - Actions you do to yourself
10. **Commands (Imperative)** - Tell people what to do
11. **Por vs Para** - Master these tricky prepositions
12. **Comparisons** - More than, less than, as...as

### Vocabulary & Communication

13. **Idiomatic Expressions** - Sound more natural
14. **Connecting Words** - Link your ideas smoothly
15. **Expressing Opinions** - Debate and discuss
16. **Professional Spanish** - Workplace vocabulary

## 💡 Study Tips for Intermediate Learners

- **Consume content**: Watch shows, read articles, listen to podcasts
- **Speak regularly**: Find language exchange partners
- **Grammar in context**: Learn tenses through stories, not just tables
- **Keep a journal**: Write daily entries in Spanish
- **Push boundaries**: Try content slightly above your level
- **Focus on fluency**: Perfection comes later
