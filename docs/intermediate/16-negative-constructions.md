# 🚫 Negative Constructions (Construcciones Negativas)

## Double Negatives Are CORRECT in Spanish -- Embrace Them!

**⏱️ Study Time: 10 minutes**
**🎯 Goal: Master Spanish negation patterns including double negatives, negative words, and common expressions!**

---

## Overview

Here's something that trips up every English speaker: in Spanish, **double negatives are not only correct -- they're required!** "No tengo nada" (literally "I don't have nothing") is perfectly good Spanish. Forget what English taught you about double negatives and embrace the Spanish way.

---

## 1️⃣ Basic Negation: NO Before the Verb

The simplest way to make something negative is to put **no** directly before the conjugated verb:

1. **No quiero** (I don't want)
2. **No tengo tiempo** (I don't have time)
3. **No he comido** (I haven't eaten)
4. **No vamos a ir** (We're not going to go)

---

## 2️⃣ Double Negative Patterns

When a negative word comes **AFTER** the verb, you MUST put **no** before the verb too. This is where the double negative comes in.

### no...nada (nothing / anything)

5. **No tengo nada**

   - (I don't have anything / I have nothing)

6. **No he dicho nada**
   - (I haven't said anything)

### no...nadie (nobody / anybody)

7. **No conozco a nadie aquí**

   - (I don't know anyone here)

8. **No ha venido nadie**
   - (Nobody has come)

### no...nunca / no...jamás (never)

9. **No he ido nunca a Alicante**

   - (I've never been to Alicante)

10. **No lo haré jamás**
    - (I will never do it)

### no...ninguno/a (none / no / not any)

11. **No tengo ningún problema**

    - (I don't have any problem)

12. **No queda ninguna entrada**
    - (There aren't any tickets left)

### no...tampoco (neither / not either)

13. **No me gusta tampoco**

    - (I don't like it either)

14. **Yo no he ido tampoco**
    - (I haven't gone either)

### no...ni...ni (neither...nor)

15. **No tengo ni dinero ni tiempo**

    - (I have neither money nor time)

16. **No quiero ni café ni té**
    - (I want neither coffee nor tea)

---

## 3️⃣ Negative Word BEFORE the Verb = No Double Negative

When you place the negative word **before** the verb, you do NOT add "no." One negative is enough:

| After the verb (double negative) | Before the verb (single negative) |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **No** viene **nadie**           | **Nadie** viene                   |
| **No** tengo **nada**            | **Nada** tengo                    |
| **No** he ido **nunca**          | **Nunca** he ido                  |
| **No** quiero **ninguno**        | **Ninguno** quiero                |
| **No** me gusta **tampoco**      | **Tampoco** me gusta              |

17. **Nadie viene a la fiesta**

    - (Nobody is coming to the party)

18. **Nada me sorprende**

    - (Nothing surprises me)

19. **Nunca he probado el pulpo**

    - (I've never tried octopus)

20. **Tampoco quiero ir**
    - (I don't want to go either)

Both forms mean the same thing. The "before the verb" version can sound slightly more emphatic or literary, but both are equally correct and common.

---

## 4️⃣ Special Rules for NINGUNO

### Apocopation: ningún before masculine singular nouns

Just like "un" shortens to "un" (wait, it already is!), **ninguno** shortens to **ningún** before a masculine singular noun:

| Wrong                | Right               |
| -------------------- | ------------------- |
| ~~ninguno libro~~    | **ningún libro**    |
| ~~ninguno día~~      | **ningún día**      |
| ~~ninguno problema~~ | **ningún problema** |

The feminine form stays the same: **ninguna casa**, **ninguna idea**.

### Ninguno is almost always SINGULAR

Unlike English where "none" can feel plural, ninguno/ninguna is almost always singular in Spanish:

21. **No tengo ningún amigo aquí** (not ~~ningúnos amigos~~)

    - (I don't have any friends here)

22. **No hay ninguna tienda abierta** (not ~~ningunas tiendas~~)
    - (There are no stores open)

## 5️⃣ Common Negative Expressions

| Spanish             | English                      | Example                                                               |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ni siquiera         | not even                     | **Ni siquiera me ha llamado** (He hasn't even called me)              |
| ni hablar           | no way / out of the question | **¿Conducir hoy? ¡Ni hablar!** (Drive today? No way!)                 |
| para nada           | not at all                   | **No me gusta para nada** (I don't like it at all)                    |
| en absoluto         | absolutely not               | **¿Te molesta? --En absoluto** (Does it bother you? --Absolutely not) |
| ya no               | no longer / not anymore      | **Ya no vivo allí** (I don't live there anymore)                      |
| todavía no / aún no | not yet                      | **Todavía no he terminado** (I haven't finished yet)                  |

## 🏖️ Valencia Daily Life Examples

23. **No conozco a nadie en el colegio todavía**

    - (I don't know anyone at the school yet)

24. **Nunca he visto las Fallas en persona**

    - (I've never seen Las Fallas in person)

25. **No tengo ni idea de dónde está el Mercado de Colón**

    - (I have no idea where the Colón Market is)

26. **¿Paella con chorizo? ¡Ni hablar!**

    - (Paella with chorizo? No way! -- a Valencian would be horrified)

27. **Todavía no hablo valenciano, pero ya no me da miedo intentarlo**
    - (I don't speak Valencian yet, but I'm no longer afraid to try)
